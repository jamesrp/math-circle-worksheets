# Activity catalog

Every theme in the library, 101 in all, grouped by the area of its central idea. Built from [catalog/](catalog/README.md) by `python3 catalog/build.py`; edit the records there, not this page. The explorer (`explorer/index.html`; download it and open it in a browser, or see the published copy linked from the [catalog README](catalog/README.md)) shows the same records with filters, the concept map and the links between activities.

Bands are entry points by prerequisites, not age limits. Status: piloted means a classroom report exists; organizer-approved means the organizer reviewed and promoted it; everything else is unpiloted.
