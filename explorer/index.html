Math Circle Explorer

Each box is a concept; arrows run from what children usually meet first to what builds on it. Choose a concept to light up its prerequisites and what it leads to, and the activities about it.

Each dot is a theme, coloured by the area of its central idea; lines are the links written in the catalog. Choose a theme to see its neighbours.

One strand per area, read left to right: concepts that need nothing earlier come first, then the ones that build on them. Each theme sits under its central concept, with the bands that have pages; “also in” lists themes that touch the concept on the way. Use it to plan a return visit or a route across years.
