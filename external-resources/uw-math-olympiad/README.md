# UW Math Olympiad problem sets

57 original PDFs from 15 competition years (2010–2019 and 2022–2026), including the additional/extra problem sets linked by the official site.

Sources: [2026 Olympiad](https://sites.math.washington.edu/~mathcircle/olympiad/) and [prior-year archive](https://sites.math.washington.edu/~mathcircle/olympiad/archive/). Downloaded 2026-09-10.

Files are organized by year and retain their original filenames. The competition was called the Math Hour Olympiad before 2025. The official archive reports that the 2020 and 2021 competitions were cancelled due to COVID-19.
