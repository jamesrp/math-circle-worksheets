# Mathematics atlas reference inputs

`MSC_2020.csv` is the official Mathematics Subject Classification 2020 download from [msc2020.org](https://msc2020.org/MSC_2020.csv), retrieved 25 September 2026. Despite the extension, it is tab-separated and Windows-1252 encoded. It is stored unchanged. See `manifest.json` for its SHA-256 hash and the counts actually imported.

The classification is jointly published by Mathematical Reviews and zbMATH under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). Preserve that attribution and license when redistributing the source or its normalized taxonomy. The atlas is our independent selection and educational design; the publishers do not endorse it. The normalized taxonomy and classification-derived coverage tables retain the same license; this notice does not relicense other books or sources in the repository.

The official landing page describes the original release as 63 fields, 529 three-character classes and 6,022 five-character classes. This download has different finer-level counts. We pin the actual bytes and report their counts rather than silently substituting the landing-page totals.

Downloaded authored sources, if any, belong here. Our summaries, source locators, mathematical proofs, activity designs, and review records belong in `plans/atlas/`.
