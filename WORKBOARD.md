# Work board

One line per item. Review cards follow [plans/review/README.md](plans/review/README.md). Claim an item by changing its line to your name, branch and state, and push that change to `main` before you start. A rejected push means someone moved first: fetch, re-read this board and choose again. A claimed item's files belong to its owner until it merges; then mark it done. Add a line for new work the same way. The plan and its order are in the app repository's [docs/plan/](https://github.com/jamesrp/small-math-adventure/tree/main/docs/plan); the app keeps its own board.
