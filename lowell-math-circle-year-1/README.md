# Lowell Math Circle — year 1

The organizer's 2025–26 material, preserved in its original folders:

- `lowell-math-circle/`: the eleven Fall 2025 DOCX handouts and two pattern-block PDFs.
- `subtract-shapes/`: the original shape-subtraction LaTeX, PDFs, and existing compilation files.

These are prior materials to consult when planning for returning children. A saved worksheet does not establish attendance or mastery by every child. Record remembered use in the [session use log](../plans/fall-k-5-year-a-use-log.md).

Newly produced worksheets belong in [year 2](../lowell-math-circle-year-2/README.md).
