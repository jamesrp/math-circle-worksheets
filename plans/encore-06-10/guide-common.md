Shared editorial specification for separate adult guides

Each guide is five short pages: theorem-first destination; practical launch/readiness; one checked solution page per investigation. Grade bands are invitations based on prerequisites. Adult reading, moving a LAST card, or scribing an experiment must not take over the child's mathematical choices. Start with handling materials, then one legal attempt together. Suggested 15–30-minute blocks describe starting routes, not a one-hour maximum or completion quota. Preserve unfinished constructions, mark which exact examples were used, and choose another investigation on a return visit.

The library's base packets stay available as independent starting points. Every companion is a prepared, unpiloted draft; no classroom observations or physical-fit rehearsal are claimed. Three anchored adults for eleven children KK11 / 3333 / 445 is the current staffing context, with two pairs at younger tables and rotating third/referee role at upper table. New older-only entries need no forced K–1 equivalent; a younger table can repeat useful concrete base work or use the new compatible acted game.
