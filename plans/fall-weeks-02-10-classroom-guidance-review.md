## Evidence and scope

The [Week 1 review](week-01-classroom-review.md) is the classroom evidence: separate starts needed too much adult management; the middle opening needed more examples; the triangle continuation was unclear; the chevron idea needed more concrete work; the pink-triangle question did not land; and the upper progression needed clearer ordering. The revisions to Weeks 2–10 are our applications of those lessons. They have not been reported tried with children.

The mathematical destinations remain: invariants and optimization, permutation cycles, modular orbits, Latin-square clues, query bounds, subtraction games, Nim, billiards, and Euler/postman routes. The work leading to them changes. More pages provide concrete comparisons, usable diagrams, and recording space; they are reserves across one meeting or later visits, not a longer assignment for the hour. Print the seven starting pages and likely continuations before the meeting. Keep other masters as shared prompts and use scrap paper if needed; on-site printing is not assumed.

## Common changes

- Student pages contain the week/topic/level header, consistent footer, and numbered problems with the rules and expected actions embedded in ordinary task text. Name/date lines, competing page titles, encouragement, and separate “go further” or “build first” labels are removed.
- Each guide starts from handling the materials and a brief demonstration with everyone: a legal action, a concrete goal, and one example of the record children will make. Different entry tasks follow that shared action.
- Purposeful contrasting cases precede classification and new notation. Maps, cycle lists, decision trees, binary bundles, unfolded tables, and pairing tables are introduced to answer questions already encountered, then used in further concrete tasks.
- Optional explanations and proofs remain available to the adult. Readiness, a satisfying stopping point, hints, and continued play matter more than finishing pages.
- New activity IDs use v3. The [use log](fall-k-5-year-a-use-log.md) keeps actual observations separate from explanations or hypotheses and from proposed revisions.

## Weekly changes

The complete tasks, solutions, and prerequisites are in each linked plan. The local README gives current page counts; the local review describes checks and remaining limitations.

| Week | Change in the concrete sequence | Depth retained in facilitator support |
|---|---|---|
| [2: switches](week-02-redesign.md) | Repeated pair flips and contrasting targets before a complete collection; paths and alternative press sets receive separate attempts. | Component parity, completeness, and shortest press sets. |
| [3: machines](week-03-redesign.md) | Repeated token moves and visible message records before cycle grouping; compare return times before designing longer ones. | Cycle decomposition, least common multiples, maximum permutation order. |
| [4: rings](week-04-redesign.md) | Trace several fixed-step routes and compare visited places before classifying; physically shuffle before position arithmetic. | Orbit length, gcd, and modular shuffle structure. |
| [5: towers](week-05-redesign.md) | Build and view contrasting skyline orders before recording visibility; make alternative cities before asking whether a clue is necessary. | Uniqueness, irredundance versus global minimum, and Latin trades. |
| [6: codes](week-06-redesign.md) | Score visible examples and compare compatible secrets before recording branches or signatures. | Worst-case identification bounds and fixed-query separation. |
| [7: take-away](week-07-redesign.md) | Play short positions and test every reply before winning/losing records; use concrete game moves before mex or finite-window notation. | Both directions of a strategy proof and eventual periodicity. |
| [8: rooks and piles](week-08-redesign.md) | Play matching and mismatched starts before marking traps; test three piles before bundling and binary records. | Two-pile matching, Nim's binary invariant, and Wythoff comparison. |
| [9: bouncing](week-09-redesign.md) | Trace contrasting rectangles before reflected copies; give unfolding its own drawings and comparisons before the general rule. | Reduced-side parity, first-corner and bounce formulas, rational directions. |
| [10: routes](week-10-redesign.md) | Walk several networks before pairing entrances/exits; physically join loops and try repeated-edge routes before optimization. | Euler existence and obstruction, splicing, and shortest closed delivery routes. |
