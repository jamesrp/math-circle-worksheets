# Student introduction audit, Weeks 1–75

**Complete for the actual current weekly print inventory:** 237 PDFs, comprising 176 base packets, 56 bonus/encore/return-visit or extension packets, and 5 materials-only PDFs. All 75 weeks and every existing band/companion opening were covered. Reviewers actually viewed 571 distinct rendered pages, including later band entries and relevant procedure introductions. Eleven investigation PDFs have localized concerns across eight issue families; 221 had no actionable introduction defect found under their normal launch assumptions. These are desk-review judgments, not observed classroom failures.

The most useful changes would complete four specific bridges: tiling to cube piles, two groups to their shared region, a prize/ticket state to both host outcomes, and a triangle point to its distance from the other two sides. Two other fixes simply move existing instructions to their first use. None calls for deleting the advanced mathematics or adding a separate printable.

## Where a normal demonstration is sufficient

The review did not equate “no printed worked example” with “unclear.” Week 23’s guide demonstrates swapping, keeping already ordered/equal cards, an untouched lane and a two-bar run. Week 49 K–1’s guide compares real towers and preserves the entire old ring while constructing the new one. Week 58 explicitly values both unchanged trays using one observer’s preference card, then repeats with the other. Week 60 deliberately saves a labeled total, erases the score fields, and reuses the same cards for the second plan. These remain **clear with the specified launch**, rather than compulsory edits.

Two lower-confidence watch items remain: Week 27 grades 4–5 p1 lacks the younger packets’ printed two-sided stability check, but the common launch supplies it; Week 67 p1 carefully states three simultaneous pair tests but demonstrates just one pair. Keep one unchanged candidate visible through the complete check during launch. Optional printed reminders could help portability, but neither warrants an automatic redesign.

## Representative positives

- **Week 1 base K–1/2–3 p1:** the physical filling goal and usable outlines make the action evident. A worked answer would add little.
- **Week 14 return visit p1:** original shape → erased corner/sides → retained result teaches ear peeling without supplying the target collection.
- **Week 20 K–1 p1 and 4–5 p1:** zero remains a participant, and simultaneous conditions are checked on one unchanged state.
- **Week 30 all base bands p1:** a weight placed beside the target visibly explains a nonobvious legal balance action and why it works.
- **Week 47 all base bands p1:** cube stacks → one marker per column → number row preserves meaning across representations.
- **Week 53 p1/p5 and Week 54 p3–4/p7:** purchases, exchanges, joins/splits and row-to-column conversions receive a meaningful intermediate step at first use.
- **Week 65 p1–2 and Week 66 p1:** movement and its consequence are visibly separate steps; children can then choose routes independently.
- **Week 72 p1:** EENE tracks position and memory separately. Signed arithmetic from p2 is an explicit readiness gate in the guide, not a reason to remove the investigation or manufacture a younger packet.
