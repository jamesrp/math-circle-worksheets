## Independent mathematics

The root checker uses direct subset enumeration, without importing the supplied checker. It checks 307 finite population/sample-size models, their full overlap support and exact mean, every printed constructive bound and witness, the repeated records, all integer budgets 2–24, and the bias counterexample. The code and results accompany this report.

General arguments were reviewed separately from these finite tests. A union has M+n−R objects; adjoining any number of unseen objects proves sharpness and the absence of an upper bound. For fixed N, choose R members of the first catch and n−R outside it; this proves exactly max(0,M+n−N)≤R≤min(M,n). Uniform n-subsets include each fixed object with probability n/N, and summing inclusion indicators gives Mn/N without independence of those indicators. Fraction matching is a heuristic inversion, not an identity or unbiasedness theorem. Moving one budget unit from the longer catch to the shorter increases the product by n−M−1 when their difference is at least two; this proves the stated balance result under its positive-integer, fixed-population assumptions.

Printed checks include lower minima 7 and 10; overlap sets {3,4}, {1,2,3,4}, {0,1,2,3,4}; upper minima 10 and 12; ratio guess 18 versus compatible total 10; five-round guesses 36,18,12,18,undefined with every N≥12 compatible; pooled ratio 22.5 versus finite-only mean 21; budget optima 6+6,6+7,10+10 with expected overlaps 3/2,7/4,25/6. Under the explicitly optional independent-round model the lower example has likelihood ratio about 1.76 in favor of 16, not certainty or a posterior probability. For N=6,M=4,n=3, all overlaps are positive yet the mean ratio estimate is 34/5, proving the caution about bias.

Lost marks on the same actual catch can only reduce detected overlap and raise a positive ratio estimate, or make it unavailable. Comparing letters and dots on that unchanged catch tests this mechanism directly. Shaking does not prove uniformity; a mark-retention control does not establish every other assumption. Those limits are retained.
