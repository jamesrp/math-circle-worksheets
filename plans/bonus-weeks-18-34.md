# Bonus/encore companions: Weeks 18-34

Current local release, October 4, 2026. **Complete: 51 distinct investigations across all 17 weeks, with 17 student companions, 17 adult guides and 17 portable source ZIPs. All material is unpiloted; physical pretests remain untested.**

Each week has three mathematical directions; purposeful contrasting examples and follow-up questions within one direction are not counted separately. These companions may support several visits. Choose one concrete entry per table and let children continue it. Finishing every page is not a meeting goal. Band labels are approximate: each adult guide states reading, arithmetic, representation and reasoning prerequisites, optional entries and readiness gates. The shared student headers select K-5 or 2-5 where useful; a separate young-child packet is not forced.

## Verification and limits

All 158 final student/guide PDF pages were rendered and visually inspected, with individual worksheet critic/reviser and guide-author page checks plus root release review. Exact final task numbering, represented diagrams, examples and conditions were independently audited. Each source ZIP was extracted into an empty temporary directory, both PDFs built there, and every rebuilt page compared with the current references for text, US Letter dimensions, off-page text and exact 110-dpi pixels. Each packaged independent mathematical checker ran in that extracted package. PDF file bytes may differ because of build timestamps; rendered pages match.

Fresh writer, adversarial critic, independent math critic and a different reviser ran for every student companion using the generated local stage files. Adult guides are a separate step: they open with precise mathematical facts and limits before individual solutions, and received independent mathematical audits including extension claims. Per-week source packages retain the actual review, final audit, guide audit, corrected answer/revision evidence, editable sources, references and generated local stage prompts. Global workflow prompt files were unchanged. Guide production is not a claim that the tested student workflow has validated adult-guide design.

The independent check scripts combine complete finite enumeration where needed with proof-derived general claims. Scripts and audits distinguish tested cases from general explanations. Trials and measured diagrams are not presented as universal proofs. Digital geometry checks do not establish counter fit, folding motion, punch capacity, fair drawing, tracing registration, staffing success or classroom pacing. Rehearse the chosen concrete procedures before teaching and record observations separately from hypotheses.

[Final consistency audit](encore-18-34/release-consistency.json) confirms that every current print PDF matches its packaged reference and the inspected author pages, and every ZIP contains the exact current source files. Current print copies, editable sources, their reference PDFs and source ZIPs are synchronized locally. Nothing was committed, pushed, uploaded to Drive or published. The existing Week 1 bonus/encore and all base PDFs/guides were left to their owners; AGENTS and global indexes were not edited by this range. The parent task coordinates the global index.
