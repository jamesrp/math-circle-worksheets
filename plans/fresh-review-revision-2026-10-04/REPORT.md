## Approved changes

- **Week 11, all bands:** one chronological firing word plus final piles; firing totals are recovered afterwards. All original tasks, chip starts and physical board dimensions remain.
- **Week 20, guide only:** K–1 begins with one-circle and inverse-build P3/P4 puzzles. Linked circles are readiness-dependent. All worked visuals remain; every harmonic condition is checked on one unchanged reference state, including zero-valued neighbors.
- **Week 24:** K–1 shares supplied pair cases and pools complete nine-case comparison pages. The guide also explicitly shares and pools Grades 2–3 P1/P3 comparisons; P2 needs short draws, not a fresh exhaustive record. Actual middle-band P1/P3 text leaves the method open and does not require each child to fill 27 cases, so those student PDFs remain byte-identical.
- **Week 33, guide only:** existing movable eight-card catalogs on p2 in every band and 32-card catalogs on p4 in both older bands already supply the rotation sort. Cards can be cut beforehand or sorted using family marks. No handwritten catalog reproduction or new student page is needed.
- **Week 35, K–1:** new one-border slide matching precedes combined glide; all six original tasks remain as P2–7. The original convention visual precedes first glide use. Existing strip dimensions remain.
- **Week 39, K–1:** new three-/four-step physical tree trips and four-step ring trips precede original longer investigations, retained as P3–7. Recording uses one ordered step tile for each actual traversal, including repeated roads. All original board geometry remains.
- **Weeks 27 and 40, guides only:** minimal concrete K–1 encounters and readiness-dependent continuation are explicit; neither band is removed. Advanced material stays available for later visits.
- **Other guides:** concise route updates name concrete return problems and fill actual staffing/preparation gaps. Existing original solution-page numbering is preserved by placing route notes at the end. Weeks 15 and 23 already offer sufficient routes; Week 15 was not redesigned. Weeks 17–19 retain their student investigations.

Independent student math reviews check the full relevant chip/deck/slide/road tasks and geometry. Enumeration checks support the examples; preserved invariant arguments explain the general results.
