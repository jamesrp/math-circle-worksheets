## The mathematics

Children count the vertices, edges and faces of closed convex solids and fold fans of face corners. Two theorems: V − E + F = 2, and the gaps (360° minus the face angles at a vertex) total 720° (Descartes). P1, P2 and P6 carry the count, and P6 shows redrawing changes neither total. P3–P5 carry the gaps, including the pyramid's unequal corners. P7 proves Euler by erasing loop edges to a tree, P8 proves 720° by counting face angles by faces, and P9 runs it backwards to the icosahedron and dodecahedron. A mathematician would enjoy it.

## App fit

Fit B, changed from C; size L. The handling is physical, but the solves below work on a screen.

- **Unfold.** Tap edges of a turning solid to cut; the app unfolds along the cuts and shows whether it lies flat in one piece. Every edge unfolding cuts a tree through all the corners: V − 1 cuts (7 on the cube), leaving F − 1 folds, so Euler's count appears from both sides. Targets: "cut the cube into this net"; the 11 cube nets with a checked "That's all". No edge unfolding of a Platonic solid overlaps (Horiyama and Shoji, 2011); compute overlaps for other solids.
- **Fans.** Drag triangle, square and pentagon corners around a point; the app folds a pointed corner, lies flat or refuses an overlap (P3, P9). Solve: every positive-gap corner from a piece set, with a checked "That's all".
- **Erase** (P7) fits Week 53's graph engine as a group.

Pitfalls: never make V − E + F or a total gap a typed prediction; show no count of nets found. Draw on P1, P3, P5, P6, P7 and P9.

**Decision, October 10, 2026.** Port, in Wave 9, with two flat groups: a corner builder where the child places triangle, square and pentagon corners around a point and the app folds a pointed corner, lies flat or refuses an overlap, with every closing ring found and a checked "That's all" and the angle total as the "can't" certificate; and erasing edges down to a tree (Problem 7) on Week 53's graph engine. Unfolding a turning solid stays with the real paper solids: a 3D view is a large build for what the nets already do in the hand. No typed V − E + F or gap predictions. The revise is for the K–1 route and doesn't hold up the port. Tracked in the app's [decisions.md](https://github.com/jamesrp/small-math-adventure/blob/main/docs/plan/decisions.md).
