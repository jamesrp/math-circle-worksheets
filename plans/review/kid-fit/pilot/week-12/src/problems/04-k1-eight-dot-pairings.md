## key
\emph{Print guide, p. 4.} There are 14 possible eight-dot pairings, listed and drawn on page 7. The task asks for as many as possible, so a well-organized partial collection is a valid stopping point. Group by 1's partner: 2, 4, 6, 8 give 5, 2, 2, 5 completions. The two pages hold 9 larger frames, fewer than the 14 pairings, so the frames do not give away the count; more go on the back. Two drawings are the same only if every dot has the same partner. A match at one dot is not a repeat: 12|34|56|78 and 12|38|45|67 agree at dot 1 but are different.
