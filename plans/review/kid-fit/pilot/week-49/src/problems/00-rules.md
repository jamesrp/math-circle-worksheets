## text @0,0 size=14 [K-1]
Stand the two old towers on an arrow side by side. Build the extra part on the new mat, at the arrow's start. Keep the old towers until all four new towers are built. Then clear the old mat, move the new towers onto it, and drop a counter in the cup.

## text @0,0 size=12
Use whole-number heights, including 0. Follow each arrow: the gap between its two old heights becomes the new height at its tail. Make every new tower before changing any old tower. An all-zero start takes 0 moves.
