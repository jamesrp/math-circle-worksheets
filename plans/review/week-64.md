## The mathematics

Opposite sides of a regular octagon are glued by translations, so a straight trip keeps its heading at every crossing. Rightward trips form two families: middle loops one width long, and outer loops whose two chords trade length (2(b − y) + 2y = 2b), equal only through the slanted edges' midpoints. All eight corners become one point with three full turns, the cone point of a flat genus-two surface. On a three-square L, one block of a whole-number direction permutes the square names, so every such trip closes or hits a corner within three blocks. A mathematician would enjoy this door to translation surfaces. P1–P3 carry the trip families, P4–P5 the cone point and P6–P9 the permutation argument.

## App fit

Proposed fit B, size M (S on a Week 41 portal engine). The row should read: 64 | Straight paths on strange surfaces | Follow straight trips through a glued octagon; sort starts by trip length; find which corners meet; trace sloped trips on a three-square L | Outer trips all have one length; eight corners make one 1080° point (genus 2); a direction's block permutes squares, so whole-number trips close or hit a corner | No (Week 41's portal board, Week 3's permutations) | B | Glue-the-squares: set where right and up lead among 3–4 squares, and the app draws trips to target return times | M.

The app makes the transfer, removing fix 1's risk. Solves:
- Glue squares so A's right-up trip returns after exactly k blocks, or A after one and B after two (P7, P8).
- Group corners by following edge matches, then claim "That's all".
- Stack sectors to count turns (P4–P5).

The certificate for "can't" comes from P9: no first return takes more than n blocks on n squares. P1–P2 suit a playground. Pitfalls: "where will it go?" predictions (the Mirror Couriers lesson), auto-coloured corner classes and visible counts.

**Decision, October 10, 2026.** Not ported in the October 10, 2026 round: its best solve, gluing three or four squares for a target return time, needs a portal board the app does not have yet; port it with Week 41's portal mat and Week 70.
