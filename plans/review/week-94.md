## The mathematics

Every report is a length plus one shared extra. Raising the lengths by t and lowering the extra by t changes no report, so reports 7 and 10 fit six worlds. In all of them Q is 3 longer than P, and one known strip fixes the extra (2–3 P2–P5). Two references fix a stretch-and-shift rule, and a third can refute it (4–5 P3–P4). With no promised form, no tests force an untested value (P5). If displays are off by at most 1, the stretch range is at most 4/D wide, so 1 and 10 is the best pair, and a repeated reference leaves every stretch possible (P6–P7). A mathematician would enjoy this identifiability. K–1 reaches only the first step: on a ruler without a zero, a length is a difference of labels.

## App fit

Not in themes.md yet. I propose Fit B, size M. The child feeds known strips to a hidden machine, then sets its dials within a request budget, and the app checks the dials. Better solves need no luck:

- Build every pair of hidden lengths that fits two reports, then declare "done" (2–3 P2).
- Choose the fewest strips that separate all 21 machines. "One can't" is certified by a confused pair for each strip: for D, (1, 6) and (2, 0) both report 12.
- Find every repair of a wrong report (2–3 P6, 4–5 P4).
- Drag a line through error bars to its steepest and shallowest fits (P6).
