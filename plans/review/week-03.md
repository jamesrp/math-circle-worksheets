## The mathematics

A machine is a permutation of n slots. Following arrows from any slot returns to it, so the slots split into loops; a block in a loop of k is home exactly at multiples of k, so the row returns after the lcm of the loop lengths. Children meet the consequences: loops of 2 and 3 take 6 turns on 5 slots; no machine moves exactly one block; 5 and 6 slots allow exactly 1–6 turns, 7 allow 1–7, 10 and 12; the record (1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 6, 12, 15, 20, 30) never drops but stalls at 6 slots; reversed arrows undo, and "undo A then B" is "undo B, then undo A". A mathematician would enjoy cycle decomposition, order as lcm, Landau's function and socks-and-shoes, all reached with cubes. K–1 P1–P3, P5, P7–P8; 2–3 P1–P2, P5, P7–P8; and 4–5 P2–P4, P7, P9 carry it.

## App fit

B, size M, confirmed. The plan's route, a group in Cup swaps, fits: a swap is a natural editor for a machine, and themes.md's "Build a machine with a given return time; run it and watch the loops light up" is the right core.

- **Board.** The mat: lettered cups on homes, a lower row showing where one turn sends each cup, arrows between. Swapping two cups in the lower row swaps two arrowheads. Run moves every cup at once with a turn counter, colours each loop as it closes, and stops when all are home.
- **Solves, checked by running.** Exactly k turns: 2, 3, 4 on four cups (K–1 P4); 6 on five (K–1 P8, 2–3 P3); 12 on seven as Hard (2–3 P8, 4–5 P4). Undo a machine already run once (K–1 P6, 2–3 P4). Undoes itself (2–3 P5). Same both ways, each moving all four cups (4–5 P8). Undo A then B (4–5 P7).
- **Find every, then That's all:** all six three-cup machines (K–1 P7); a machine per turn count on five or six cups (2–3 P7, 4–5 P3). No tally; themes.md names Week 3 here. The certificate is a shelf of loop splits with turn counts (7, 11, 15 for five, six, seven cups), which also backs "That's the most" (4–5 P9).
- **Can't:** move exactly one cup (K–1 P5); every edit moves two arrowheads, and the reveal says so.

It should not ask "how many turns?" before Run (2–3 P2 and 4–5 P2 stay on paper), colour loops while the child builds, score swaps (Cup swaps counts swaps to sort; keep the goals apart), or compose machines in the child's head. Show arrows, since undo and A-then-B goals depend on direction. Random swapping finds 6 on five cups fast (20 of 120), so Hard goals should be rarer: 12 on seven cups is 420 of 5,040; 15 on eight is 2,688 of 40,320.
