## The mathematics

Cops and robbers on a finite graph, positions visible, stays allowed, cop first. Every tree is cop-win: delete the cop's dot, and the robber's piece shrinks with each advance (shared P1–P2). Every bare ring of four or more dots is robber-win; the triangle is not (shared P3–P4). Added roads can help either side (upper P2–P3); a map is cop-win exactly when dominated dots fold it to one dot (upper P4, Nowakowski–Winkler, Quilliot); two cops win on every ring, one blocking while the other sweeps (upper P5–P6). A mathematician would enjoy it: strategy against every reply, and a classification with a checkable certificate.
