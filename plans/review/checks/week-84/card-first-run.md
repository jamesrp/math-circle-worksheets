## The mathematics

A player knows a fact when every possibility fitting their private view agrees. In the café (P1), a first NOT YET proves that person wants a cup, and after a NO every later speaker says NO whatever their card. With three hidden-to-self headbands and the public "at least one is blue", the k blue dolls prove on round k, by induction (P2 for k = 1, 2, 3; P3 over all seven deals). Without the announcement nobody ever proves, even in BBB where every doll sees two blues: each doll keeps an own-red card, so an all-NOT-YET round removes nothing (P4). A mathematician would enjoy it: possible-worlds knowledge and a fixed-point argument for "ever", on eight cards.

## Fix

1. **should**, p. 3, P3: "Put deals into groups that give the same three replies in round 1. Can later rounds separate every group?" Comparing which replies were shown, not who showed them, BRR, RBR and RRB match in every round and the answer flips from the key's "yes" to "no" (math.md item 1; my enumeration: seven histories by doll, three without). Not a must: p. 8's slots say Doll 1–3, P2 asks doll by doll, and guide p. 3's "distinct response patterns (the sole blue proves)" tells the adult which reading the key uses. Fix: "Put deals into groups in which each doll gives the same reply in round 1." Write "Start each deal" for "Start each trial".
2. **should**, guide pp. 1 and 3: nothing says what the announcement adds. In BBR and BBB every doll already sees a blue, yet without the announcement nobody proves (P4); what changes is that RRR leaves the shared set, since everyone knows everyone heard. The catalog record says so; the guide does not. Add it to the overview, and to P4's key the optional "In Deal 3 every doll could see a blue. Why did the announcement matter?" with the chain: in BBB doll 1 allows RBB, where doll 2 allows RRB, where doll 3 allows RRR. Also guide p. 1's "Later NO need not mean the later speaker personally says NO." contradicts itself (math.md item 3): write "the later speaker's own card is NO", and "cup" for "drink".
3. **should**, guide p. 2, launch: it shows only private matching. It skips the café, which opens the guide's own route, and "remove arrangements that could not give those replies", the least familiar action, shown nowhere. Fix: launch with student p. 1's example on cards; before the dolls, play one round of the two-doll deal BR. Doll 1 shows PROVE BLUE, doll 2 NOT YET; BB would give two NOT YETs and RB a PROVE BLUE from doll 2, so both leave. That shows a removal without the two-blue timing.
4. **should**, guide p. 3, P2 key: give the cards left each round, which the adult checks: Deal 1 leaves BRR; Deals 2 and 3 leave BBR, BRB, RBB, BBB, then BBR or BBB. Add hints, such as "Which of your doll's matching cards gives it red?"
