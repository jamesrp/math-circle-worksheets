## Exact rotor-road decision

For vertices 0,…,m, **m counts edges**. Both endpoints absorb; next-exit cards alternate after use, persist between chips, and each chip finishes before the next launch at fixed s. Let S be the number of next-right internal cards. Each step preserves position+S, hence `mR+S_final=ns+S_initial`. For n=m, `m(R−s)=S_initial−S_final` has absolute value at most m−1 and is divisible by m, so R=s. This applies from every initial state and to every consecutive block, with **no burn-in**. Full arrow configurations can be transient; their return is not needed for this count theorem. The separate binary-tree eight-block theorem does restore every card. Balance is not independence.
