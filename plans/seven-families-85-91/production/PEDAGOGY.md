Production began 17 October 2026 from fetched canonical main 902006a; the work-board claim was published as 6e0879c and the reviewed reserved plans recovered from local commit 5ae164a into canonical commit 8bfe422. The seven reserved numbers and historical planning records are preserved. This production is limited to Weeks 85–91. The lead read README, LOCAL-RESOURCES, current AGENTS, the workflow and its current context, the complete introduction-audit report, current source READMEs and actual opening PDF text of Weeks 77, 78, 80, 81 and 82. These calibrate concrete purpose, a complete operation with useful intermediate state, stable input under comparison, and concise task wording. This is targeted calibration, not a new audit or revision of those packets.

The lead directly reread Givental, Nemirovskaya and Zakharevich, *Math Circle by the Bay*, Preface pp. viii–x (PDF pp. 9–11), in the authorized retained archive. SHA-256 was verified against RESOURCE-MANIFEST.tsv: 84e0277cd94b1e501d153834b7123a72958f8c01b806e7bdfcca012a3191e427. The source advocates themes with depth across ages, manipulatives and independent problem solving; it emphasizes clarity and variable pace, and declines universal timing for topics. Our separate first routes, readiness gates and return visits are production decisions informed by these points. They are not claims that the source tested these seven activities.

The current workflow context is eleven children at fixed KK11 / 3333 / 445 tables with three anchored adults. Approximate grade labels do not remove reading, arithmetic, attention or spatial prerequisites. A younger hands-on station is not counted as an independent younger student packet. Adults may read and record while children choose and perform the mathematical actions.

Fresh writer, adversarial critic, independent mathematics and reviser agents produce the student pages. Theorem-first facilitator guides are a separate authoring step and receive final student–guide checking. Every delivered page and variant needs actual rendered inspection, and every portable ZIP needs a clean reconstruction and PDF comparison. These digital checks do not establish exact physical fit, adult preparation time, sustainable classroom handling or children's comprehension. All activities remain unpiloted and physical rehearsal untested until observations are actually recorded.

Downloaded references, runtime files, intermediate renders, generated stage prompts and borrowed style examples are excluded from these source archives and production commits. The retained planning evidence is historical: its old “unbuilt” statements describe the planning phase, and the production index/status identify the current printable version. GitHub publication does not imply a Drive upload.
