The source plans were located in the original isolated `plan/seven-families-85-91` checkout at commit `5ae164a`. Current main `902006a` had no delivered Week 85–91 files and did include the six completed Week 92–97 families and the latest Week 82 promotion. The lead recovered the 32 authored planning/review/check files without wholesale external references. Historical planning wording and independent-review fingerprints are retained.

The lead read `WEEKS-92-97.md` and the new-family scope report on actual current main. That six-family scope explicitly reserved 85–91; it did not have access to these detailed plans. The new production restores that missing continuity without renumbering. Weeks 98–100 are reserved to a separate research task. No claim is made of a collection-wide novelty audit.

Production preserves deep continuations and allows multiple visits. It may change the planned exact page count, examples, typography or packet split to serve the mathematical action and reduce reading. Each weekly QA/production record must identify material deviations and the actual prerequisites. No independent K–1 packet is promised simply because an adult can demonstrate the theme to younger children. A source theorem, checked finite case, experiment, conjecture and classroom observation remain distinct.

The authorized outcome includes finished review drafts, theorem-first guides, owned editable sources, portable ZIPs, verified canonical publication and one accurate print index. It does not promote them as organizer-approved or mark them taught. Physical fit/procedures/preparation time remain untested unless an actual rehearsal occurs. No Drive upload is implied.
