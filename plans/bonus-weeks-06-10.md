## Source and teaching notes

Read *Math Circle by the Bay*, preface printed pp. viii–x (PDF pp. 9–11). Its authors explicitly favour themes with mathematical depth, manipulatives, independent attempts, reserve problems and different depths for younger/older children. They say that topic duration varies by class and expect the five main themes to take at least two years. Our shared companion format, small fixed-table staffing, individual investigations and return-visit plan are adaptations; the book does not report this particular organization.

Lowell Fall 2025 *Handouts 10.docx*, second Problem 10.1, asks for shortest strings containing all two-digit passwords over three or four symbols; Problems 10.2–10.7 investigate 45-degree billiard endpoints and scaling. The new Week 10 triple/circular window task openly revisits the password theme through a directed overlap graph. Week 9 adds prescribed two-bounce wall words, a complete interior-dot classification and crossing geometry. The archived extra already tried rational slopes and one midpoint loop; those are acknowledged ancestors, not new themes. These connections are not claimed as entirely new themes for returning children. Week 21 base already contains shortest one-contact mirror routes; its return-visit companion optimizes two contacts on parallel walls. Both Week 9 and that companion use repeated reflection. Week 9's new goal is to enumerate rectangle wall words, reject corner hits and distinguish traversal order; reflection itself is not counted as new. See `week-21/week-21-bonus.pdf` for the related optimization direction.

Current Week 6 fixed-position code and question limits remain available. New Week 6 deliberately excludes Hamming-distance codebooks because current Week 18 already contains those investigations. Week 7's ordinary subtraction, misère subtraction, periodicity and two-pile games; Week 8's ordinary Nim, binary proof and queen game; and Week 10's undirected classification, repairs, loop splicing and minimum repeats remain base material rather than being counted again. Week 10 facilitator page 7 already names directed balance, Fleury and de Bruijn extensions; the new companions turn those seeds into usable investigations, rather than claiming those directions were absent.
