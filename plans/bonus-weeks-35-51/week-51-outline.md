Week 51 bonus: Honest measurement ranges

Mathematical kernels

1. For nonnegative independently admissible rectangle sides A in [a,b], B in [c,d], area is in [ac,bd], and both endpoints are attained. Joined/perimeter intervals alone can lose dependence. For whole side lengths with A+B=6 and A,B>=1, areas are 5,8,9,8,5; the independent [1,5] product bound is safe but loose. Tile rectangles give a concrete entrance to nonlinear/dependent bounds.

2. Two guaranteed ranges for the SAME unchanged hidden length combine by intersection, not by addition or averaging. Disjoint guarantees are inconsistent. Multiple ruler/sleeve clues can produce a strongest feasible range and require detecting an erroneous clue. No probabilistic distribution or ruler-resolution error is inferred.

3. For endpoints with overlapping length ranges, the absolute distance has minimum zero if a shared value is feasible and maximum at a pair of extreme endpoints. If the ranges are disjoint, the minimum is the gap between them. Comparison may be guaranteed, possible, or impossible. Choose a substantial adversarial near/far or ordering design task in which strips can cross in length; the base subtraction assumed a longer strip throughout.
