# Weeks 98–100: verified release

October 17, 2026. [Print and source index](../../../lowell-math-circle-year-2/WEEKS-98-100.md).

The approved final topics are bootstrap percolation (98), optimal transport (99) and ultrametric geometry (100). The release contains **eight PDFs, 47 pages, three editable source folders and three portable ZIPs**. Weeks 98 and 99 have Grades 2–3 and Grades 4–5 packets; Week 100 has a Grades 3–5 packet. Each family has a separate theorem-first facilitator guide. No K–1 coverage is claimed.

## Verification and limits

- Fresh writer, critic, mathematical review and reviser stages were completed separately for the student packets. Guides were authored separately and each complete student–guide family received an independent final review. Guide authoring remains an untested addition to the tested student workflow.
- Every one of the 47 final pages was rendered and visually inspected. Mathematical claims, worked examples, solutions and final problem references were checked together. Detailed review reports are retained separately and are not included in this public release.
- Executable independent mathematical checkers ship in each source package. Builders reject TeX errors and overfull boxes. US Letter geometry, embedded fonts, text extraction and local index links are checked.
- Each portable ZIP is clean-extracted and rebuilt using only its own sources and documented dependencies. Every rebuilt PDF must match both the delivered PDF and packaged reference PDF by SHA-256.
- [Release audit](release-audit.json), [output/index checks](output-index-checks.json) and [verification script](verify_release.py) record reproducible digital evidence. Per-family manifests: [98](week-98/release-check.json), [99](week-99/release-check.json), [100](week-100/release-check.json).

**Prepared for organizer review; unpiloted.** Topic approval and publication approval do not establish final design approval or classroom success. Physical printing, material fit, preparation, handling, staffing and pacing remain unrehearsed. The guides propose first visits and return routes rather than promising complete packets in an hour. Breadth closes at 100; Week 83 and the older combined sets were not changed.

## Source recovery and provenance

The cloud workspace reset during publication. Accepted PDF blobs and source packages were recovered without changing their bytes. Every student/guide TeX file, builder and mathematical checker was recovered exactly against the pre-reset Git-blob manifest. Week 99's source README, public release metadata and indexes were reconstructed; the three source ZIPs were repackaged after metadata updates. These are newly validated package hashes, not a claim that the archives match the pre-reset ZIP bytes. The final accepted PDFs were not redrafted.

Packages exclude downloaded works, borrowed workflow exemplars, prompts, raw session records, detailed review reports and build caches. Sources and overlap limits are documented in each package's SOURCES.md and the [original research record](../RESEARCH.md). No private reference or network access is required to rebuild.
