# New themes 52–63: production and verification

The current release table is [WEEKS-52-63.md](../../lowell-math-circle-year-2/WEEKS-52-63.md).
This production counts twelve themes. Grade versions and return visits are
tracked separately. Each theme offers several investigations and possible return visits.
All twelve selected themes are complete: 28 PDFs / 233 pages, twelve portable editable source directories and twelve matching source ZIPs. The index links every current local deliverable. Mathematics and every final page received independent review; every delivered PDF reproduced exact text, dimensions and pixels from a clean extracted source ZIP.

## Selection and distinctness

[NOVELTY-AUDIT.md](NOVELTY-AUDIT.md) records the mathematical destination and
nearby prior work for each selected theme. Selection used the actual current
base/bonus indexes, extracted headings/text from 309 existing PDFs, and targeted
reads/renders of relevant prior pages. This is not a claim that every old page
received a new mathematical or visual review. The earlier atlas supplied leads;
it is not a vetted curriculum or evidence of piloting.

The new work includes genuine continuations from familiar objects where the
mathematical question differs. Pick's theorem was already mentioned in Week 31's
bonus adult material, spherical octants/lunes appeared among atlas leads, and
the three-card derangement entry appears in Week 43. Their new themes develop
full polygon area/additivity, spherical triangle geometry/area, and overlap
counting/recurrence respectively. Ramsey, prefix codes and de Bruijn were not
counted again as new themes.

## Production record

[stage-status.json](stage-status.json) identifies completed and pending work.
Researched kernels and physical constraints preceded outlines and the unchanged
prompt generator. Student stages use separate writer, adversarial critic,
enumeration/math reviewer and reviser agents. Separately authored guides receive
an independent coordinator review; the coordinator authored neither their
student pages nor their guides. Approximate bands reflect actual prerequisites;
no equal three-band page quota was imposed. Guides retain concrete starting
routes, deeper explanations and return options.

Each released bundle contains current student PDFs, any needed original material
PDF, a separate theorem-first facilitator PDF, lean editable sources, original
source/provenance notes and a ZIP. Downloaded references stay in
`external-resources/new-themes-52-63/`; stage files and renders stay under `tmp/`.
No prompt exemplars, reference-book pages or third-party reference PDFs are
included in the new source ZIPs. Established mathematics and actual teaching
precedents are cited; adaptation choices are distinguished from those precedents.
Root `REPUBLISHING.md` is a separate task and was preserved.

## Verification evidence and its limits

[final-visual-review.json](final-visual-review.json) records actual final-file
hashes and every student, material and guide page inspected by the coordinator.
Separate stage verifiers check enumerations, examples, polygon side counts,
equal scales, original figure geometry and portable builds. Compact stage reports,
guide review decisions and independent coordinator calculations are retained in
each plan folder's `reviews/`. Original stage-local evidence and render/build
intermediates remain in their week-local runs under `tmp/worksheet-runs/`.

Each released week's `release-checks.json` records a fresh extraction of its
actual delivered ZIP, build from a separate directory, and exact page-text,
dimensions and rendered-pixel agreement for every delivered PDF. Released PDFs
are copied byte-for-byte from the inspected final versions. A later TeX/font
version may render differently; the recorded equality applies to the production
environment. Relocated source instructions and ZIP contents are synchronized.

An independent bracing-guide review found an additional valid refit in the open
student task: coincident corners can make a doubled triangle after an unbraced
collapse. The guide now explains it and distinguishes local continuous rigidity
from distant equivalent embeddings; the student's question remains open.

The lattice-area mathematics review also repaired a preliminary proof gap:
an arbitrary triangle's bounding-rectangle complement need not be three right
triangles. The current notes and guide route use two legal diagonal cuts of a
lattice quadrilateral, then polygon-ear induction. Concrete student areas were
correct. Historical generated prompts remain unchanged and the correction is
recorded explicitly rather than attributed to a source that did not supply it.

[final-release-audit.json](final-release-audit.json) checks actual released PDF
hashes/page fingerprints, complete coordinator page coverage, every source ZIP
against its editable directory, and all local links in the new index.

[preservation-check.json](preservation-check.json) compares exact bytes of the
309 inventoried current PDFs and ten unchanged workflow inputs. It does not
inventory every historical source file or make claims about pre-existing user
edits. Existing weeks, bonus content, global indexes, `AGENTS.md` and original
extras have not been rewritten by this production.

**Physical fabrication, print-fit measurements on real materials, handling and
staffing rehearsals, and classroom piloting remain unperformed.** Every guide
states its required pretests and honest readiness gates. Digital checks establish
mathematical and document consistency, not operational classroom readiness.
These are local releases; no commit, push, remote upload or publication occurred.
