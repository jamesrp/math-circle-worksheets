# Novelty boundaries for new themes 52–63

October 4, 2026. These are new themes relative to the current 51-theme weekly library and its 153 bonus investigations. The earlier 90-topic atlas supplied unvetted leads; reuse of a mathematical subject mentioned there is acknowledged in each source note, not counted as proof of a suitable elementary activity.

The audit inventories 309 current direct week-local PDFs across all 51 themes (206 student/preparation packets and 103 adult guides, 1,828 pages). It text-extracts all current PDFs and reads the two current indexes. Detailed topic checks inspect the relevant actual student and adult pages and source notes. The inventory is not a claim of a new line-by-line mathematical review of every old page. Old archives and remote copies are outside this novelty audit.
