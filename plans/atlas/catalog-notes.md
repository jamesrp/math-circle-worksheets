# Notes from the catalog pass (October 23, 2026)

Three readers wrote a catalog record for each of the 90 families (`catalog/atlas/`), reading the JSON card, the printed student pages and the facilitator guide side by side. These are the differences they found between the cards and the print books. Nothing here was changed in the cards or the books; the catalog takes titles and page ranges from the print books and shows card text only for the adult question, the anchor theorem, the gates and the materials.

## Titles

The print books retitle most families (21 of the 24 AD families, and many GA and AP ones). The [page finder](worksheet-expansion/INDEX.md) and the random-ten builder hold the printed titles, and the catalog uses those.

## Different examples on the printed pages

The worksheet expansion develops new instances on purpose, so a card's launch or worked example can differ from its pages. The readers found these:

| Family | Card | Printed pages |
|---|---|---|
| AD-15 | a circle | an ellipse |
| AD-16 | y² = x³ − x | y² = x³ + x + 1 |
| AD-22 | a square with diagonal AC | a square with a centre point |
| AD-24 | title "Necklaces that resist dividing by four" | six beads, three red |
| AP-03 | (card example) | six scores, three trained |
| AP-06 | shares GA-28's x³ − 2x + 2 and its 0 ↔ 1 cycle ([connections](connections.md), row GA-28 and AP-06) | x³ − 5x, a 1 ↔ −1 cycle; GA-28 keeps x³ − 2x + 2 |
| AP-21 | 3 days, 2 energy tokens | 5 days, 5 battery tokens |
| AP-23 | four-unit costs, where the cheapest arrangement is also stable | costs 1, 3 and 5 |
| AP-25 | starts at (3, 4, 0) | starts at (4, 7, 1) |
| GA-25 | reuses AD-05's five-edge square-with-diagonal graph ([connections](connections.md), first row) | a nine-room grid |
| GA-05, 16, 18, 20, 21 | card examples | other examples |

The AP-06, AP-21 and AD-24 rows were rechecked against the PDFs; the others are as the readers reported. Two rows of `connections.md` (GA-28 with AP-06, and AD-05 with GA-25) describe a shared example that the printed pages no longer share.

## Gates

- AP-07's pages need derivatives, though its card's entry gate is F/A.
- GA-03, GA-11 and GA-15 are recorded as 4–5 only and GA-07 as beyond K–5, which is stricter than their entry letters suggest; the readers judged from the printed pages.
