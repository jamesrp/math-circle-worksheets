# Search frontier and what 'complete' can mean

An atlas can have a complete *index* without having completed the mathematics. This edition pins all 6,603 rows of the official downloaded taxonomy. The top-level survey is a breadth pass. The 534 subarea rows, 5,503 ordinary subject rows, and 503 cross-cutting rows remain individually unvisited in the coverage ledger until a later record justifies another status.

## The next unit of research

Take a subarea, read an authored overview and at least one precise problem/theorem source, and record 3-5 *distinct questions*, not synonyms for the field. For each question, try a finite object, lower dimension, restricted parameters, geometric model, part of a proof, or genuine symbolic investigation. Preserve failed bridges and the reason they fail. A failed search is useful, but a quick failure is not a theorem that no accessible entrance exists.

Use these dispositions per explicitly investigated anchor: candidate; exact special case; faithful representation; shared mechanism; motivation only; prerequisite stop; unresolved; duplicate with a reason. A field label and a source citation are not themselves a disposition for its descendants.

## Protected searches after the breadth edition

| Territory | Specific work still needed | Why a small existing example does not finish it |
|---|---|---|
| Logic and foundations | Model theory, proof theory, computability, set theory, independence | Truth tables do not instantiate independence from an axiom system. |
| Number theory | Analytic distribution, algebraic integers, local fields, Diophantine geometry | Modular clocks do not cover analytic number theory or arithmetic geometry. |
| Abstract algebra | Modules, representation theory, homological constructions, noncommutative phenomena | A group action or matrix example has a limited anchor. |
| Algebraic geometry and K-theory | Schemes, sheaves, intersection theory, vector bundles and higher invariants | A polynomial plot or formal difference is an entrance to one construction only. |
| Continuous analysis | Measure, convergence modes, differentiation, singularities, function spaces | Finite counting, numerical samples and smooth examples miss pathologies. |
| Geometry and topology | Curvature, Riemann surfaces, manifolds, homotopy, homology, characteristic classes | A cut-and-glue surface does not establish the classification theorem. |
| Differential equations | Existence/uniqueness, weak solutions, shocks, stability, boundary conditions | A recurrence model has to earn any claimed continuum limit. |
| Harmonic and spectral analysis | Approximation, Fourier convergence, uncertainty, infinite spectra | Decomposing four numbers does not prove Fourier-series convergence. |
| Probability and statistics | Conditional models, dependence, estimation, identifiability, experimental design, causal assumptions | Dice experiments do not by themselves justify an inference. |
| Algorithms and complexity | Reductions, undecidability, randomized algorithms, approximation, lower bounds | An inefficient puzzle solution does not prove a complexity lower bound. |
| Applied mathematics | Model derivation, dimensions, conservation, inverse problems, parameter estimation | A visually similar toy is not automatically the physical equation. |
| Mathematical physics | Relativity, quantum theory, statistical mechanics and fields | Classical probabilities are not quantum amplitudes; a rubber sheet is not spacetime. |
| Interfaces | Tropical geometry, optimal transport, topological data, information geometry, machine learning theory | Intersections deserve searches, not only multiple labels on one card. |

## From families to a six-year circle

The elementary goal remains twenty sessions a year for six years. Ninety atlas families across all prerequisite levels would **not** equal ninety elementary sessions, and a hundred parameter changes would not automatically equal a hundred worthwhile investigations. Build six-year routes only from activities whose gates fit the actual children, allowing genuinely different revisits and several sessions for rich families.

For a proposed return, record the previous exact instance and what changes: the object, allowed action, target question, proof, or connection. A changed context alone does not reset prior use. Retain a reserve of unused instances, and record what children actually did rather than marking the whole packet 'covered'.

The next production selection should deliberately include: a non-counting concrete investigation, a continuous one, a statistical inference question, a symbolic algebra question, and one genuinely calculus-gated investigation. That selection tests the atlas across its intended range. Classroom feedback then informs which other families deserve detailed student pages.

## Review limitations

Computational checks certify only their explicitly enumerated domains. A rigorous general argument is recorded separately. Agent review is mathematical/editorial review; it is not a human elementary educator's assessment or a classroom pilot. Source access failures remain visible rather than being converted into verified citations. No current research problem is declared 'still open' merely because it appears in an older source.
