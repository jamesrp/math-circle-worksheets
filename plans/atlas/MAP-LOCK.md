# First-edition territory map lock

The first breadth survey is locked on 25 September 2026 as **atlas map v1**. Lock means stable field ownership and IDs for the next design pass, not mathematical infallibility or a claim that all interesting problems have been found. Review may correct claims and future editions may add anchors.

All 63 top-level fields in the pinned official MSC snapshot have one substantive dossier: 16 in algebra/discrete/foundations, 27 in geometry/topology/analysis, and 20 in probability/computation/applications. Fields 00, 01 and 97 have explicit cross-cutting research/support dispositions. The other 60 receive student-investigation development, at the actual prerequisite level the mathematics warrants.

The coordinator read the three surveys and reconciled headings against the imported code list. The independent undergraduate-syllabus check is in `reviews/coverage-audit.md`. The result supports proceeding with the breadth design pass. It does **not** support marking any of the 534 subareas or 6,006 five-character entries complete. In particular, a source's table of contents establishes scope; only inspected relevant text or a supplied self-contained proof supports the specific theorem used in a family.

## Design assignments

| Volume | Family IDs | Target | Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Algebra and discrete mathematics | AD-01 onward | 24 families | All 14 substantive fields; protect noncommutative, homological and K-theoretic gates. |
| Geometry and analysis | GA-01 onward | 36 families | All 27 fields; genuine continuous questions and honest SCV/global-analysis stops. |
| Probability and applications | AP-01 onward | 30 families | All 19 substantive fields; distinct inference, physical models, control and strategic games. |

The 90-family target is an editorial breadth allocation, not a count of distinct mathematical disciplines or a promise of 90 elementary sessions. Each record follows `family-format.json`. A direct-source check and the exact example are author obligations; independent review follows. A family may use a shared mechanism as long as its limitation is explicit. Do not turn a difficult topic into a falsely exact simple analogy to fill a quota.

Priorities beyond field counts: learner choices, surprise, a question that rewards explanation, low-preparation objects, and extensions that change the mathematics. The existing Weeks 1-10 remain a quality benchmark and duplicate-check corpus. Their topic labels are not reused as credit for new work.
