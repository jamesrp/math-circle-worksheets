Local first-year sources: `lowell-math-circle-year-1/lowell-math-circle/Math Circle Fall 2025/Handouts N.docx`. They were read as historical inputs, not silently corrected. For example, Handouts 3 has a winning/losing labeling slip in its introductory prose; the atlas should use the corrected current game notes when it needs that result.

For every future session, record family ID **and exact instance**. A revisit counts as progress when at least one of these changes substantially: objects, legal moves, mathematical question, explanation, or connection. Merely increasing all numbers, changing a story, or printing the same board for an older group is not a new investigation.
