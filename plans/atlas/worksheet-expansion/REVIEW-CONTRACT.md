# Independent review and release gates

The author must not be the only mathematical or PDF reviewer for a batch. The editor assigns a different author to each review. Review other authors' files read-only, send concrete findings promptly, and let the owner make repairs.

## Mathematical/design review

Read all eight families, every student prompt, all keyed answers, guide-only extensions, source evidence and mathematical check code. Independently solve the chosen instances before comparing the key. Check the proofs as arguments, including zero/endpoint/degenerate cases, quantifiers and omitted hypotheses. Running the author's checker is useful support, not independent proof.

For each family record:

1. Which prompts and mathematical claims were checked, and an independent calculation or derivation that makes the check reviewable.
2. Whether the proposed exact diagram, board, data table or legal moves are sufficient for the task.
3. The substantive payoff and actual prerequisite gate; flag a warm-up being marketed as a substitute for the adult idea.
4. Any ambiguous scope, answer leak, accidental repeated problem, inaccessible instruction or unrealistic preparation.
5. Concrete repairs with family/prompt IDs, followed by closure after rereading the repaired text.

Review all families, including the difficult ones. Do not infer correctness from the original atlas or replace a hard family with a simpler unrelated puzzle. Save `review-<batch>-design.md` and any genuinely useful independent check program/results beside the data.

## PDF review

Inspect **every student page at full size**. Inspect every guide page at least on contact sheets, with full-size inspection of dense math, diagrams and any suspect page. Read the actual student instructions and verify that every necessary object, rule and working area is present. Assess whether a learner can experiment and make choices without an answer being printed prematurely.

Check geometric fidelity, crossing/adjacency/scale/arrow conventions, distinction between supplied scaffolds and things to construct, clear variable/axis labels, sources, prerequisites and prompt/key alignment. Mechanical blank-page/bounds/glyph reports supplement visual review; they do not certify diagram meaning or detect every overlap.

Save `review-<batch>-pdf.md`, listing the actual preview path and page coverage, concrete findings and their disposition. Inspect repaired pages under **new paths**: the image viewer can cache an earlier PNG at the same filename. Do not claim the entire final assembled book received a new visual pass if only the reviewed page bodies and new contents were checked.

## Editor release checks

- Exact eighty-family set, disjoint from the original ten; all original source hashes unchanged.
- Every planned student prompt printed exactly once and answered; every extension answered.
- Actual prerequisite gates visible, including calculus for GA-08.
- All meaningful mathematical check programs pass and independent proof findings closed.
- All student pages and all guide pages covered by the specified visual review.
- All final pages render; no blank pages, out-of-page text or suspect glyphs.
- Contents links/bookmarks point to the correct family starts.
- Final assembled page bodies match reviewed previews, with changed pages re-reviewed.
- Accurate source/output hashes, page index, first-pilot assessments and remaining limitations.

There is no classroom-pilot gate imposed on this authorized production task. Mark the results unpiloted and document what was verified; do not stop work to request a pilot or approval.
