# Worksheets for the remaining eighty atlas entries

All eighty remaining atlas families now have student investigations and separate facilitator solutions. The six books contain **245 student worksheet pages, 497 prompts and 85 solved extensions**, plus contents and facilitator material. Every batch has closed independent mathematical and PDF reviews. The [page finder](INDEX.md) lists exact print ranges, prerequisites and candid teaching judgments for each investigation.

The original [random-ten books](../worksheet-trial/README.md) remain unchanged. Together these two projects cover the ninety atlas family IDs. This is a selectable library across many prerequisite levels; it is not an exhaustive inventory of mathematics or an established six-year elementary sequence.

Use the [page finder](INDEX.md) or each book's linked contents and bookmarks to select a family. Print the selected student pages single-sided, US Letter, at Actual Size; keep the guide separate. Hand out one page at a time so a later example or result does not spoil an earlier discovery. Later pages and extensions can be another meeting. Consult the guide for materials, preparation, launch, timing, hints, complete reasoning and satisfying stopping points.

Choose by prerequisites. Some investigations begin with counting, drawings and movable objects; others genuinely require algebra, calculus or advanced concepts. GA-08 retains calculus, as requested. An accessible first page does not establish readiness for every continuation. The index distinguishes promising first pilots, investigations needing specific preparation, and advanced investigations.

The design aims to continue the revised Week 1 standard: meaningful choices, useful representations, a substantial mathematical payoff and a checked explanation. The assessment for each family is an editorial judgment. These activities remain **unpiloted**; timing, preparation estimates and sustained engagement have not been verified in a classroom. Record actual use only after children encounter an investigation.

The work had stopped partway through production. At recovery, 32 families had closed mathematical and PDF reviews (`ad1`, `ga1`, `ap1`, `ga4`), another 24 had approved designs and partial PDF production (`ad2`, `ga2`, `ap2`), and the final 24 remained to develop (`ad3`, `ga3`, `ap3`). ## Teaching basis

The editor reread *Math Circle by the Bay*, preface printed pp. viii–x / PDF pp. 9–11, for this expansion. Its documented lessons include deep themes, manipulatives, variable pace, independent attempts and repeated opportunities to explain. Our page counts, batch organization and particular problems are editorial choices. Exact source locators and source-versus-inference distinctions are in [lesson-format-source-notes.md](../../lesson-format-source-notes.md).
