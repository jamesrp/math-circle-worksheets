# Independent plan review protocol

Review the learner rules and exact instance before reading the supplied solution. Work the small problem independently, then compare. A source citation is not a substitute for checking that the card's assumptions match its theorem.

Review each assigned card on four dimensions:

1. **Mathematics:** instance is well-posed; answer correct; completeness or optimality justified; boundary actually tests an assumption. A numerical simulation is not a general proof. Distinguish a failed proof from a false claim.
2. **Bridge:** identify counterparts of the learner objects, legal actions and question. Exactness is scoped to the specified special case. A physical observation, finite analogue or advanced continuation is labeled accurately.
3. **Access:** exact reading/arithmetic/abstraction requirements; a meaningful entry action; enough time to explore; hints reveal progressively; the satisfying stop is possible without the advanced theorem. A supplied rule can be followed before its justification is mastered, but that distinction must be visible.
4. **Usefulness and novelty:** practical material/preparation; learner decisions; a real question rather than copying or listening; substantial difference from existing Weeks 1-10 and neighboring cards. A symbolic advanced card is acceptable when its prerequisite is genuine.

Write a report with every reviewed ID and findings, distinguishing blocking mathematical errors, substantive clarifications, and optional improvements. Include the exact corrected statement or instance for every material finding. Save executable enumerations and exact checks under `plans/atlas/reviews/` with the domain tested. Do not silently overwrite another author's source; the editor applies or commissions repairs and records closure.

Do not declare classroom suitability validated. This is agent mathematical/editorial review, not observation of children or a human educator review. All actual-use rows remain blank until real sessions occur.

## Review depth

Every exact small instance and explanation is to be checked. Sample source citations for the crucial nontrivial theorem claims and revisit any doubtful hypothesis; report which could not be independently verified. A general research continuation beyond the proved instance may remain a proposed direction. Do not label the entire card 'fully source verified' when only its finite computation was checked.

## PDF review

After the plan is repaired, verify that PDF text preserves the latest exact rules and answers. Render all pages. Check font coverage, clipping, page flow, headings, page numbering, source links, bookmarks and page references. Inspect contact sheets for every page and full-size images for dense/formula/table pages. Report any limitations; do not infer visual quality solely from successful text extraction.
