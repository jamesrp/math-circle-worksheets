# Source grounding and limits

The actual current PDFs were fully inventoried, rather than treating an earlier pass as coverage. Text extractions are working records in `tmp/encore-01-05/`. The existing Week1 encore is our format/use model; it stays current and untouched. The new companions deliberately branch to different questions.

*Math Circle by the Bay*, Preface printed pp.viii-x (PDF9-11): the authors describe selecting themes with deep mathematical context that can develop across age levels, using manipulatives and dialogue, and avoiding fixed estimates for topic duration. Their multi-year scope is directly stated in the source. Applying those lessons to these five three-investigation companions and choosing optional return visits is our inference, not a classroom observation from the source.

Prior-year *Pattern Block Exercises*, pp.1-2: the organizer's earlier worksheet uses green/purple/yellow/blue/red filling tasks and classifies leftover triangles by symmetry. New Week1 companion investigates perimeters, local corner closure and global seam-pattern symmetry rather than those prior instances.

The exact finite mathematics is independently checked in `verify_kernels.py` and `kernel-checks.json`. These checks do not test manipulative fit, child reading load, engagement or staffing in a real meeting. All companions remain unpiloted; no physical rehearsal is claimed.
