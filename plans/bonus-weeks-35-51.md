# Weeks 35–51 bonus / encore inventory

Seventeen themes, **51 distinct investigations**. Each investigation is counted once, regardless of examples, entry routes, bands or possible visits. Three student pages per week form one selected-band companion, with a separate adult mathematical guide and a portable source ZIP. These are return-visit choices; finishing a whole packet in an hour is not expected. Grade labels are approximate; the listed prerequisites and guide entry routes control suitability. No separate K–1 packet is forced where the mathematical entry does not work.

**Status: unpiloted. Physical material fit, cutting, model handling, mixing and procedural rehearsals remain untested. Current locally only; no Drive upload or publication.** Base student PDFs, base facilitator guides, Week1 encore, AGENTS.md, combined sets and global indexes were outside this range's ownership and were not edited by this task.

## Models, sources and review process

The actual Week1 encore source/packet and the actual current base student PDFs in every band plus guides for Weeks35–51 were inspected before design. Prior-year Handouts10 (logic, codes and billiards) was consulted for the organizer's concrete puzzle style. Base extraction records are in `tmp/bonus-weeks-35-51/base-week-NN.txt`. No Week1 bonus investigation was replaced or copied.

Downloaded pedagogy consulted: *Math Circle by the Bay*, Preface, printed viii–x (PDF9–11), describes manipulatives and independent work (ix), deep themes extending across at least two years and class-dependent timing (x), and improving explanations through experience (ix). Its authors also say they rarely repeat a topic for the same children. Our return-visit organization is an inference from flexible depth and this group's needs, rather than a source claim that repeated encore sessions were their practice. *Paths in Mouseville*, printed69–73 (PDF82–86), informed Week50's drawing/listing routes before recurrence; the new obstacle boards are original. Each guide records any additional mathematical source inherited from its base theme and distinguishes new extensions.

The checked-in worksheet workflow generated each outline/stage set. Distinct writers, adversarial reviewers, independent mathematics reviewer and fresh revisers processed every packet. The runtime selected-band layout adapter is `tmp/bonus-weeks-35-51/WORKFLOW-ADAPTER.md`; the canonical prompt set was preserved. The adult guides are a separately reviewed, untested authoring step. Per-week outlines and independent exact checks are in this folder; stage reports live in `tmp/worksheet-runs/week-NN-bonus-v1/`.

## Related return paths and use record

Weeks35/49 revisit finite-state repetition through footprint constraints and differences, respectively. Weeks37/40 concern invariants under allowed physical transformations, with different objects and rules. Weeks41/50 connect recoverable move words and paths, but the portal board is periodic while the staircase/obstacle board is bounded. Weeks42–45 give distinct probability directions: extracting fair outputs, producing shuffles, changing a bag by feedback, and reasoning from partial evidence. Week46 concerns reset/copy transformations, while Week47 concerns constrained height landscapes.

Week48-B2 and Week51-B2 both combine guarantees: the former concerns area-cover certificates and constructible regions; the latter diagnoses false length reports with witnesses. Week48-B3 and Week50-B3 both show that visual proximity does not bound length: the former preserves enclosed area and connected geometry while changing perimeter; the latter permits finite retracing inside a narrow corridor and varies total travel length. These links are opportunities for comparison rather than extra investigation counts.

For each actual visit, record date, chosen investigation IDs, prerequisite readiness, concrete objects used, children's attempts and conjectures, exact explanations reached, confusion, and where to resume. Distinguish observations from hypotheses, and digitally verified material from classroom-tested revisions. No prior use of these new investigations is claimed.
