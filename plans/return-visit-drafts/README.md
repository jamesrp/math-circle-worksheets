What each visit is about, and what the reviews changed:

- **Week 53: dearest links, paid trips and optional places.** Routes whose most expensive link is as cheap as possible, and opening links in price order while ringing the groups the pawn can walk between (every cheapest network contains such a route for every pair, and the groups give the cheapest cost without choosing a network); paying for a network once against paying for a trip every time, including what adding the same amount to every price does to each; and optional places drawn as squares, which a network may use or leave out (K–1 compares the cost with and without the square). After review, the grades 4–5 general questions say "on a map with no squares", since the packet's own square maps were counterexamples to them; price labels that sat nearer a neighbouring link were moved; children draw or write the networks they find, not only their costs; and K–1 Problem 7's wording was made unambiguous.
- **Week 58: exact halves and fair thirds.** Trains of red and blue cards shared between two trays with as few cuts as possible (with two colours, two cuts are always enough), a red and blue strip shared exactly between two different tastes, and the marking game for three people on the X, Y, Z panels, with why it gives everyone at least a third. The reviews' three must-fixes are in: every strip cut now goes straight across, so a lengthwise cut no longer solves the strip problems; the train rule says a tray may get more than one piece; and K–1 counts places where a train is pulled apart, not motions. Grades 4–5 now have a worked example of the marking game before first use.
- **Week 62: allowed slots, single moves and arrows.** Lists of allowed slots on each circle (when two numbers per circle are not enough, as on an odd ring with the same two numbers everywhere, or where two circles are each joined to the same four), changing one counter at a time from a start to a goal (including rings where no counter can move), and arrows that force an order (the longest arrow path decides how many headers are needed). After review, impossible move tasks are questions ("Can you…?") rather than commands, each packet's opening rules show the four headers as the colour key, the stuck-ring problems require different numbers at the two ends of every line, and grades 4–5 meet stuck rings before the trapped five-ring.
- **Week 63: swaps, match totals and new forbidden homes.** The fewest swaps that bring a row home (cards minus loops), counting cards at home across every outcome card two ways (the total always equals the number of outcome cards), and new boards of crossed-out homes, counted by hand and then without listing. After review, K–1 has a written rule and a worked swap before the first problem, grades 2–3 Problem 9 is no longer answered by Problem 8, K–1 Problem 5 includes the right answer among its choices, and in grades 4–5 the shaded grids get a worked example and four-card boards before the five-card counts.

## Facilitator guides and review

Each folder contains `facilitator.pdf`, portable `guide-src/`, the original `review-guide.md`, and `guide-revision.md` with every finding's disposition, mathematical checks, final-PDF problem/page map, material counts and student-page workarounds. The [pinned run index](https://github.com/jamesrp/math-circle-worksheets/blob/24a18cb02a3f45c91311c90d5a85b65886450278/plans/guide-runs/README.md) preserves the original drafts and independent critic scripts; no run folder or machine-local prompt is included in the main tree. All required guide findings were addressed. Minor concision limits are explained in the individual reports.

What the guide critics caught and the revisers changed:

- **Week 53:** added discoveries by band, the missing K–1 square comparison and upper trips/squares to flexible first/return routes, explicit price conventions and child explanations; removed duplicated narration. The final guide is seven pages. See [review](week-53/review-guide.md) and [dispositions](week-53/guide-revision.md).
- **Week 58:** added the two-taste/two-cut result and band discoveries, a run sheet and exact print plan, fractional-mark guidance and clearer band labels; shortened repeated exposition. See [review](week-58/review-guide.md) and [dispositions](week-58/guide-revision.md).
- **Week 62:** replaced the undersized printed launch with a non-task 25 mm hand-drawn board, added an adult K–1 move record and operational prerequisites, made contrasting first/return routes explicit, and shortened repeated proofs. See [review](week-62/review-guide.md) and [dispositions](week-62/guide-revision.md).
- **Week 63:** fixed both must-fix answer leaks: supply surplus counters rather than exactly six, and give each middle pair its own unmarked outcomes sheet. Added concrete split/join reasoning, checkable completeness/count arguments, a self-contained parent page and realistic later routes. The cutting plan now counts two young-pair outcome sets and four extra uncut sheets. See [review](week-63/review-guide.md) and [dispositions](week-63/guide-revision.md).

Student pages are unchanged. Their guide-stage workarounds remain important:

- **Week 53:** an adult convention fixes invented-price domains and strict/tied wording. A squared-price road costs 36, above the 30-counter kit; write oversized totals instead of treating a kit shortage as impossibility.
- **Week 58:** upper Problem 6's “whatever values” requires each person's original total divided by three, not always 4. Some middle marking games require a 112.5 mm quarter-panel mark. Train/strip pictures and spare rows do not prescribe physical lengths or an answer count; keep the two-person and three-person value cards separate.
- **Week 62:** the upper top-row allowed-slot labels are close; read the actual labels together. An indirect arrow path also forces distinct slots. The guide's enlarged demonstration is separate from the student's working boards.
- **Week 63:** match counters may obscure tiny cells, so point/tally or circle a fresh whole sheet. Keep complete outcome decks and each pair's sheet separate; blank rows do not promise an answer count. The guide records the finite board-domain and overlap-count conventions.

These are digitally checked drafts, not evidence of successful print fit, cutting, staffing or pacing. No kit has been rehearsed and no child trial is claimed.

## Rebuilding

Each `src/` folder rebuilds its three PDFs with Python 3 and pdfLaTeX, run inside `src/`:

- Week 53: `python3 build.py` writes the PDFs into the draft folder. `python3 verify.py` re-checks every answer.
- Week 58: `sh build.sh` writes the PDFs into `src/`. `python3 check.py` re-checks every answer (about five minutes).
- Week 62: `sh build.sh` copies the PDFs into the draft folder. `python3 instances.py` re-checks every answer by brute force.
- Week 63: `python3 build.py` writes the PDFs into the draft folder. `python3 verify.py` re-checks every answer.

On October 25 each draft rebuilt from a clean copy of its `src/`, with text identical to the committed PDFs, and each checker passed.

To rebuild a guide, run `sh guide-src/build.sh` from any of the four draft folders (or `sh build.sh` inside `guide-src/`). It writes `../facilitator.pdf`, rejects LaTeX errors/overfull boxes and ends with its checker reading the three unchanged student PDFs beside `guide-src/`. Dependencies: Python 3, pdfLaTeX with the packages listed in `guide-src/README.md`, and Poppler. Copy the whole draft folder outside the repository to test portability. All four packaged builds passed, with PDF bytes and extracted text identical to the pinned revision outputs; see [verification](../guide-packaging-verification.json).

## After approval

Assign the return visit to its week (`lowell-math-circle-year-2/week-NN/week-NN-return-visit.pdf` is the Weeks 1–51 pattern) and add it to [the return-visit index](../../lowell-math-circle-year-2/BONUS-AND-RETURN-VISITS.md). Store it as [problem files](../../lowell-math-circle-year-2/source/problem-files/README.md). Review and adapt the packaged draft facilitator guide when promoting the approved pages; retain its theorem-first overview and unpiloted status until classroom evidence exists. Update the week's catalog record (`catalog/weeks/`), removing its `[[drafts]]` entry for these pages, and run `python3 catalog/build.py`. Then record it as unpiloted.
