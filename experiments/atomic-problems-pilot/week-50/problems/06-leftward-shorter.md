## prompt @180
Can a path with a leftward piece use less string than a staircase?

## figure
calibration(225)

## key
A positive leftward piece cannot make a path shorter than 280 mm. Every millimeter left must be recovered by an extra millimeter right, adding two millimeters to the baseline. A route with any positive leftward travel is strictly longer than a staircase.
