## prompt
Find this path's exact length. It goes left once. Explain which part of your argument about right-and-up staircases no longer applies.

## figure
left_once(42)

## key
The printed left-once route is Route A in the key to \emph{Paths that may go left}. Horizontal travel is 80+40+120=240 mm and vertical travel is 40+40+40=120 mm, giving \textbf{360 mm}. Net horizontal displacement remains 160, but horizontal distance traveled is no longer 160; this is the exact part of the staircase argument that changes.
