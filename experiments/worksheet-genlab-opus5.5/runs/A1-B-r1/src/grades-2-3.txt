Bellingham Math Circle · Week 7 · Grades 2–3               Name


  Two players share one pile of counters and take turns. On each turn a player
  takes away 1, 3, or 4 counters, and nobody may take more counters than the
  pile has. Whoever takes the last counter wins.
  A pile is a losing pile if the player whose turn it is will lose when the other
  player plays carefully. A pile is a winning pile if the player whose turn it is can
  win, whatever the other player does.


Problem 1: Play the game several times with a pile of 6 counters, taking turns
going first. Which player can always win from 6, the first player or the second
player? Write how that player wins.
