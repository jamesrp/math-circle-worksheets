  A take-away game uses one pile of counters and a set of allowed moves, such as
  {1, 3, 4}. Two players take turns. On each turn a player removes a number of
  counters that is in the set, and no more than the pile has. Whoever takes the last
  counter wins.
  A pile is a losing pile (L) if the player about to move will lose when the other
  player plays carefully. Every other pile is a winning pile (W). A pile of 0 is a losing
  pile, because the other player has just taken the last counter.

description is correct for piles of every size.
