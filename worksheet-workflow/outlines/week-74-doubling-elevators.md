Week 74: Doubling elevators

Mathematical kernels

At nonnegative level h, a horizontal left/right move changes ground coordinate x by 2^h and costs one; up/down changes h by one and leaves x fixed. Start (0,0), finish (16,0). Eight moves suffice: up twice, right four times, down twice. Any route of at most seven moves reaching maximum height H uses at least 2H vertical moves, hence has displacement at most (7-2H)2^H. For H=0,1,2,3 the bounds are 7,10,12,8, all below 16. This is a concrete exponential shortcut/subgroup-distortion model related to BS(1,2), not the full Cayley graph. Restricting a finite board or drawing only nonnegative levels must not be misrepresented as the whole group. Routes may go left or change levels more than once.

Sources: https://ntouikan.ext.unb.ca/MATH6022/IntroCGGT/html_output/section-15.html (Baumslag–Solitar distortion); https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~kapovich/EPR/ggt.pdf (distortion, printed p. 285; PDF p. 305).

Suggested emphasis by level: one shared Grades 4–5 prototype, by prerequisites. Concrete paired exploration first; proof and generalization late. No forced K–1 or separate Grades 2–3 version. These are new, unpiloted prototypes for organizer review.

Materials

Per pair: elevator road board with clearly marked level and ground coordinate, pawn, move cards or a single recorded move word. All levels align to the same horizontal coordinate scale; an up arrow never silently changes x. If high-level ticks skip labels, vertical connections still must land at the same x. Use a enough-width working strip; no physical specialist apparatus.

Prototype scope

Aim for about four focused student pages, each with a substantial investigation; a justified shorter or longer packet is fine. Include unfamiliar-convention visual examples before first use, but do not give away the main discoveries. Keep state recoverable and bookkeeping light. Use partner enforcement of rules, clear next actions, no generic encouragement or decorative flourishes. Honest prerequisite gates matter more than nominal age.
