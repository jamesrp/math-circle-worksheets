Week 75: Twists on a cylinder

Mathematical kernels

An annulus is a cylinder with two boundary circles. A Dehn twist fixes both boundary circles pointwise but adds one winding to a strand joining corresponding fixed endpoints. In angular/height coordinates, (theta,t)->(theta+2*pi*t,t) is such a map: at t=0 and t=1 the boundary points are unchanged modulo 2*pi. Composing twists adds winding; inverse twists cancel. Relative winding is visible after cutting the cylinder open and lifting to repeated rectangles. For two simple joining arcs with identical endpoints and winding numbers a,b, the minimum number of interior intersections is max(|a-b|-1,0), when shared endpoints are excluded; different endpoints change this formula, so do not guess from a picture. Keep endpoint/rim markings fixed, distinguish strand relaying from twisting a whole surface, and do not use a fake paper demonstration where simply rotating a ring undoes the intended effect.

Sources: https://dianadavis.github.io/billiards-book.pdf (printed p. 111, Problem 111; PDF p. 129: torus shear and reassembly). This provides twisting/unfolding lineage. The fixed-boundary annulus model and interior-intersection formula are independently derived extensions, not claims attributed to that exercise.

Suggested emphasis by level: one shared Grades 4–5 prototype, by prerequisites. Concrete paired exploration first; proof and generalization late. No forced K–1 or separate Grades 2–3 version. These are new, unpiloted prototypes for organizer review.

Materials

Per pair: ordinary paper cylinder with marked rims and seam, yarn or narrow removable paper strips, tape, and cut-open/repeated rectangle template. Use a workable strand-routing model with fixed taped endpoints and winding counted via seam crossings, or another physically accurate model. A rigid paper cylinder cannot literally undergo a smooth twist without deformation: describe the model honestly. Do not require untested specialized hinged sleeves. Physical rehearsal will remain explicitly untested.

Prototype scope

Aim for about four focused student pages, each with a substantial investigation; a justified shorter or longer packet is fine. Include unfamiliar-convention visual examples before first use, but do not give away the main discoveries. Keep state recoverable and bookkeeping light. Use partner enforcement of rules, clear next actions, no generic encouragement or decorative flourishes. Honest prerequisite gates matter more than nominal age.
