Week 85: Drawing machines

Mathematical kernels

1. Ideal pantograph with midpoint bars O-X-Q and Q-Y-P, all four sides of the simple noncrossed rhombus X-Q-Y-T equal L, gives P=O+2(T-O). In vectors from O, X=u,Q=2u,Y=2u+v,T=u+v,P=2u+2v. This proves collinearity and doubling for every noncollapsed intended pose.
2. Corresponding segments double, angles and orientation are preserved, and area multiplies by four. Swapping input/output gives factor one half. Holding tracer path fixed while translating anchor by d translates the output by -d. These are meaningful separate return visits; preserve all three directions, without assigning all work to one hour.

Materials

Provide actual-size adult drilling/cutting templates, topology, and joined two-sheet 432x279 mm mat with calibration segment and registration/overlap guidance.
