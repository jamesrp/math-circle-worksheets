Week 79: Numbered-ball detectives

Mathematical kernels

At completed day n the smallest-label transfer rule gives A={n+1,...,2n}, B={1,...,n}. Both have n balls. Label k enters at ceil(k/2) and is permanently in B by day k. Pointwise membership tends to empty A and all positive labels in B, while counts grow. Moving the larger new label instead leaves odds in A and evens in B with the same finite counts. Infinite sum and pointwise limit cannot generally be interchanged. These are stipulated eventual-membership limits, not a final day.

Sources: Organizer’s process; directly derived finite formulas and limits. Related existing series/limits activities Week50 and atlas GA12.

Suggested emphasis by level: Grades 2–3: enact and adopt labels; Grades 4–5: contrast counts, labels and changed transfer rule. No K–1 edition.

Materials

Two shallow bowls per pair, 24 supplied numbered paper cards at least 25mm square, A/B placement mats, adult-scribed common observations. Large enough mats for twelve cards; no concurrent student tallies.

Draft scope: one selected-band students/materials PDF; substantial independent investigations, essential worked visual before unfamiliar action, large reusable props, no printed theorem answers on student boards. Preparation, optional prompts and mathematical follow-ups belong with adults.
