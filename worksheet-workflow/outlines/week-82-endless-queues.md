Week 82: Endless queues

Mathematical kernels

Order-preserving bijections distinguish cardinal size from well-order shape. Prepending one to omega permits star->1, n->n+1, so 1+omega=omega. Appending gives a last element, omega+1, unlike omega. Two consecutive omega blocks have a boundary element with infinitely many predecessors and no immediate predecessor, unlike endlessly many finite pairs (2*omega=omega). Both two-block and interleaved orders have countable cardinality. Ordinal sums are concatenation; surreal addition is commutative and must not be conflated.

Sources: Hamkins, Counting to Infinity and Beyond, https://jdh.hamkins.org/counting-to-infinity-poster/ . Original matching activity rather than copied poster.

Suggested emphasis by level: Grades 3–5 only: reading schematic endless strips, rules for every customer, order-preserving matches. No forced younger variant.

Materials

Numbered cards 1–12, star card, two colors of continuing-row strips, yarn, A4/Letter reusable schematic order boards. Continuation mark is an instruction, not customer. An after-every-numbered panel must be explicit; do not put star just after the drawn finite prefix.

Draft scope: one selected-band students/materials PDF; substantial independent investigations, essential worked visual before unfamiliar action, large reusable props, no printed theorem answers on student boards. Preparation, optional prompts and mathematical follow-ups belong with adults.
