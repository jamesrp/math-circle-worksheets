Week 80: Midnight laboratory

Mathematical kernels

Switch at times 1-2^(-n), alternating post-switch ON/OFF. Times approach 1; binary states have no left limit. The pre-endpoint prescription alone admits either endpoint value as an added rule; requiring continuity is impossible. Motion halfway toward a fixed point converges. No last switching mark exists. Finite physical play illustrates rules, not actual supertasks.

Sources: Thomson (1954), Tasks and Super-Tasks; Benacerraf (1962), Tasks, Super-Tasks, and the Modern Eleatics. Original classroom adaptation of organizer’s lamp example.

Suggested emphasis by level: Grades 2–3: folding and alternating; Grades 4–5: comparison of processes and endpoint conventions. No K–1 edition.

Materials

Long paper strip or joined sheets, two-sided ON/OFF card, a movable counter, ruler, reusable timeline. At least 180mm working length; only first six halvings are meant for physical marking, later ones imagined from rule.

Draft scope: one selected-band students/materials PDF; substantial independent investigations, essential worked visual before unfamiliar action, large reusable props, no printed theorem answers on student boards. Preparation, optional prompts and mathematical follow-ups belong with adults.
