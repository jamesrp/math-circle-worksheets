Week 73: The gentlest stretch

Mathematical kernels

Compare a side-marked 4-by-1 rectangle and a 2-by-2 square with Euclidean distances. Among homeomorphisms carrying each named side to its corresponding side, the smallest possible worst pairwise distance ratio is 2. Lower bound: a unit vertical segment in the rectangle joins opposite horizontal boundaries, whose images are at least 2 apart in the square. Upper bound: (x,y)->(x/2,2y) stretches every pair by at most 2 and preserves the required sides. Sparse pin measurements give only lower bounds; a proposed map needs a rule for the whole sheet. This is a Lipschitz comparison, not the Teichmuller metric. A child-controlled model should let children choose and test correspondences or stretch designs; avoid making this an adult lecture with decorative rubber bands.

Sources: Metric comparison inspired by https://people.reed.edu/~davidp/talks/talks05-06/talks/duchin.html (Moon Duchin, November 3, 2005, Billiards and Geometric Topology). The elementary side-preserving Lipschitz calculation is developed here; no claim it occurred in the talk.

Suggested emphasis by level: one shared Grades 4–5 prototype, by prerequisites. Concrete paired exploration first; proof and generalization late. No forced K–1 or separate Grades 2–3 version. These are new, unpiloted prototypes for organizer review.

Materials

Per pair: side-marked rectangle/square drawings to scale, movable labeled paper dots, ruler, thread lengths or paper strips for distance comparison, and blank grids for children’s alternative maps. Do not rely on stretching real rubber uniformly. Provide a workable paper-based mapping game with clear legal conditions; adult notes must honestly distinguish finite samples from a whole continuous map.

Prototype scope

Aim for about four focused student pages, each with a substantial investigation; a justified shorter or longer packet is fine. Include unfamiliar-convention visual examples before first use, but do not give away the main discoveries. Keep state recoverable and bookkeeping light. Use partner enforcement of rules, clear next actions, no generic encouragement or decorative flourishes. Honest prerequisite gates matter more than nominal age.
