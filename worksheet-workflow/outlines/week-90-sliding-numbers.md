Week 90: Sliding number arrays

Mathematical kernels

1. An ordinary skew Young diagram in English orientation is a left-justified outer diagram with nonincreasing row lengths, minus a similar upper-left inner diagram. Distinct labels1...n increase rightward and downward. Start a hole at a removable southeast corner of the missing inner diagram; slide in the smaller immediate right/below numbered neighbor (only one if sole); repeat to an outer corner, then delete the exit square. This preserves row/column increase and label count; southeast hole motion proves termination.
2. Ordinary jeu de taquin rectification does not depend on successive legal inner-corner choices, including the final LABELS, not merely shape. Choice is between complete slides; each inner slide is deterministic. General theorem needs advanced proof; small full catalogs offer finite proofs and broader experimental evidence. On (3,2)/(1) there are five increasing four-label starts; output shapes(2,2) or(3,1). On (3,3,2)/(2,1) there are16 five-label starts and final shapes(3,2),(3,1,1),(2,2,1), with two possible full corner orders. On diagonal (3,2,1)/(2,1), six fillings yield four distinct outputs with multiplicities1,2,2,1: 123 yields vertical column,321 horizontal row;132 and312 yield [1,3]/[2];213 and231 yield[1,2]/[3]. Children may divide cases and pool checks, not transcribe sixteen boards individually.
3. With original corner AND exit cell retained, reverse a single slide using the larger immediate up/left neighbor until the marked original corner. Final rectification without this history can have many preimages. Changing forward choice to larger can break increasing order. These give new encore directions without recopying the full catalog.
