Week 83: Hackenbush balances

Mathematical kernels

Normal-play finite red/blue stalks grounded at bottom have dyadic number values. Blue removes a blue segment, Red a red; segments cut off from ground vanish; one component per move, no move loses. Bottom-to-top B=1,R=-1,BR=1/2,BRR=1/4,RB=-1/2. Two BR and one R form zero, two BRR and one RB form zero. Zero means previous-player win, not draw; finite tests motivate equality but full-context equivalence is a theorem. Optional simplicity cuts choose earliest-born allowed value: {0|3}=1,{-1|1}=0,{0|1}=1/2,{1/2|1}=3/4. Finite birthdays give dyadics. Infinite cuts are teacher-led doors, no ordinal addition on student pages.

Sources: Schleicher and Stoll, An Introduction to Conway’s Games and Numbers, https://arxiv.org/html/math/0410026v2 , Sections2–3. Independently authored stalk boards.

Suggested emphasis by level: Grades 2–3: legal cutting and balance play; Grades 4–5: strategies and optional earliest-born nursery. No K–1 edition.

Materials

Red/blue magnetic segments or dry-erase diagrams, clear ground, moves checked by partner. Draw segments at least 22mm and mark B/R for color-independent reading. Large reusable empty grounded board. Optional number/birthday cards spanning day0–3 and explicit left/right mats.

Draft scope: one selected-band students/materials PDF; substantial independent investigations, essential worked visual before unfamiliar action, large reusable props, no printed theorem answers on student boards. Preparation, optional prompts and mathematical follow-ups belong with adults.
