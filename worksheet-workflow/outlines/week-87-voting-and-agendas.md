Week 87: Voting and agendas

Mathematical kernels

1. Three fixed sincere rankings ABC/BCA/CAB yield A>B, B>C, C>A each 2:1. Two options compete, winner faces waiting third option: each candidate can win by waiting. A candidate beating both others wins all agendas. Changing order never changes cards; sincere pair votes choose the higher AVAILABLE option, even if favorite is absent.
2. Five voters with fixed distance favorites p1<=...<=p5 on one common line have strict majority winner p3, if available. Against any q<p3, last three strictly prefer p3; against q>p3, first three do. Equal-distance ties among other voters do not spoil three strict votes. Repeated favorites count separately and position zero is a participant. Example favorites 0,2,4,7,10: winner 4; vs 0 it is (3 for 4,1 for 0,1 tie); vs 8 it is 3:2.
3. Return visits: four favorites 1,3,7,9 have weak winners [3,7], integers 3..7 on 0..10 board, but no strict universal winner. The cyclic three-card profile cannot be realized by distance preferences for three distinct candidate positions on a common line: whichever candidate is in the middle cannot be anyone's strict last choice, but each is last on one card.

Sources: Duncan Black, On the Rationale of Group Decision-making (1948), II pp26–28; Stodder, Strategic Voting and Coalitions (2005), classroom Condorcet game; Acemoglu MIT 14.770 slides 20–23. Reusable knockout mat approx175x100 mm with first pair, winner, waiting option, final pair/winner and loser/out space. Six blank ranking cards with same grid for child-designed profiles, erasable letters sufficient. Provide actual cut sheets/mats as materials PDF, not a list of absent printables. Five favorite markers 12–14 mm at distinct parking rails over a 0..10 line with 16 mm tick spacing (160 mm span); two different-shape proposal markers X/Y below. Stacked identical favorites retain all voter identities on separate lanes. Votes move to tally wells while favorites stay fixed. Extra V5 removed for four-voter route. No concurrent distance table, rankings transcript and tally.
