Week 66: Lamplighter streets

Mathematical kernels

A state is both the walker's integer position and the finite set of lamps that are on. One move is left one, right one, or toggle the lamp at the current position. Starting all off at position 0, reaching lamps {-1,0,1} on with walker back at 0 takes 7 moves: 3 toggles and at least 4 walks. From that target, each of its three neighboring states has distance 6 from the start. Thus every legal next move can move closer to the original start. Do not confuse distance to the target with distance from the all-off start. For a target lamp set and final position, the fewest moves equal required toggles plus a shortest line walk visiting those lamps and ending at the required position. This is a concrete lamplighter-group word metric, not Week 2's edge-toggle invariant.

Sources: https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~kapovich/EPR/ggt.pdf (Exercise 7.82, printed p. 233; PDF p. 253); https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0309344 (dead-end examples, PDF p. 9).

Suggested emphasis by level: one shared Grades 3–5 prototype, by prerequisites. Concrete paired exploration first; proof and generalization late. No forced K–1 or separate Grades 2–3 version. These are new, unpiloted prototypes for organizer review.

Materials

A numbered line mat, one clearly different walker pawn, and two-sided lamp counters large enough to flip. Per pair: 9 counters for positions -4 through 4, one pawn, pencil. A partner checks that only the lamp under the pawn can flip and preserves the pawn in the final-state diagram. Record one move word if needed, not simultaneous tallies.

Prototype scope

Aim for about four focused student pages, each with a substantial investigation; a justified shorter or longer packet is fine. Include unfamiliar-convention visual examples before first use, but do not give away the main discoveries. Keep state recoverable and bookkeeping light. Use partner enforcement of rules, clear next actions, no generic encouragement or decorative flourishes. Honest prerequisite gates matter more than nominal age.
