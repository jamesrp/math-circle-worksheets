Week 81: Buckets of Fish

Mathematical kernels

Remove exactly one counter from a nonempty bucket and optionally add a finite number only to buckets strictly earlier (left). Finitely many buckets and finite refills imply every play ends, by induction on rightmost bucket or decreasing ordinal omega^(m-1)*a_last+...+a_first. Starting (0,1), arbitrary finite first refill gives no uniform finite length bound. All-even states are losing under normal play; move in the rightmost odd bucket and repair earlier parity. Capped warm-up permits at most one refill per earlier bucket; parity strategy still works.

Sources: Joel David Hamkins, Buckets of fish!, https://jdh.hamkins.org/buckets-of-fish/ . Game attribution, original boards and task sequence.

Suggested emphasis by level: Grades 2–3: play capped game; Grades 4–5: uncapped finite refill and termination versus unbounded duration, optional parity. No K–1 edition.

Materials

Three large left-to-right bucket zones per pair; thirty loose counters; extra supply off-board. Use counter-sized spaces not decorative bucket interiors. Quantity cards for a large finite imagined refill; cap actual warm-up at one per earlier bucket.

Draft scope: one selected-band students/materials PDF; substantial independent investigations, essential worked visual before unfamiliar action, large reusable props, no printed theorem answers on student boards. Preparation, optional prompts and mathematical follow-ups belong with adults.
