## Week 2 shared collection

The [shared student collection](week-02/week-02-shared.pdf) has **42 pages and 50 problems**, with one [adult guide](week-02/week-02-shared-facilitator.pdf). It replaces the separate Week 2 grade packets and K–1 auxiliary. Begin with the common lamp action, then choose from making and walking puzzles, collecting states, shorter solutions, drawing rooms, and networks. The [shared plan](../plans/week-02-shared-collection.md) and [preparation notes](source/week-02/README.md) give prerequisites, page choices, solutions, and coverage for ten children with three adults.

The separate [compact puzzle catalog](week-02/week-02-shared-catalog.pdf) has **4 pages** of small start-to-target lamp diagrams, with unnumbered opening guidance and ten consecutively numbered problems. It keeps the original target-image scale, uses brief task captions, and ends with a partner-puzzle prompt. The full collection and combined packets remain available in their existing formats.

The optional [Fast Finisher / Bonus Challenges](week-02/week-02-bonus-challenges.pdf) adds **four investigations on four pages** for children ready for another question after either catalog: do-nothing moves, bridge predictions, a solo switch, and unequal prices. Print the separate [two-page adult key](week-02/week-02-bonus-facilitator.pdf) for solutions and hints. [Design notes and prerequisites](../plans/week-02-bonus.md). Prepared September 30; unpiloted reserve.

Give only a few selected pages at a time. Familiar examples may be skipped after a couple of successful attempts; the drawing route is available immediately. Keep the three adult-led tables stable while sharing pages across ages. Paper and pencil/eraser or whiteboards are sufficient. These are unpiloted revisions. The seven preceding PDFs are preserved in [the pre-shared archive](week-02/archive-before-shared-collection-2026-09-28/).

## Editing and rebuilding

The [ten random atlas investigations](../plans/atlas/worksheet-trial/README.md) are a separate worksheet trial: [student book](combined/atlas-random-ten-student-worksheets.pdf) and [facilitator guide](combined/atlas-random-ten-facilitator-guide.pdf). They range from concrete networks and games to calculus, with explicit prerequisites. They are not assigned to weeks; use the contents to print a selected investigation.

The [remaining eighty investigations](../plans/atlas/worksheet-expansion/README.md) continue that library across algebra/discrete mathematics, geometry/analysis, and probability/applications. Each subject has separate student and facilitator books; the [page finder](../plans/atlas/worksheet-expansion/INDEX.md) links all six PDFs and gives exact ranges and prerequisites for selected investigations. [Expansion build instructions](source/atlas-remaining/README.md) describe their editable sources and review workflow.

The [mathematics atlas](../plans/atlas/README.md) adds two separate facilitator planning books: [investigation plans](combined/math-atlas-investigation-plans.pdf) and [research map](combined/math-atlas-research-map.pdf). These cover many prerequisite levels and are not weekly student packets. Use their page contents and bookmarks to print selected investigations. [Atlas build instructions](source/atlas/README.md) describe their separate data-driven builders.

Edit the worksheet sources under `source/week-NN/`, then run that folder's build script. Week 2 combines ReportLab and LaTeX sources. It writes to the corresponding `week-NN/` print folder and leaves compilation files in the project's `tmp/` folder. Rebuild combined packets after changing an individual week. See [build commands and dependencies](source/README.md).

Downloaded originals belong in [external-resources](../external-resources/README.md); the organizer's 2025–26 material stays in [year 1](../lowell-math-circle-year-1/README.md).
