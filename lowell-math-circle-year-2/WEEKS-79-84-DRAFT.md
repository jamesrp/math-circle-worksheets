Grades 2–3 concrete core; Grades 4–5 continuation. **80. Midnight laboratory** — Fold a timeline, flip ON/OFF, and move a counter halfway. Explore what the rule says about the endpoint. Grades 2–3 manipulation and rule design; Grades 4–5 endpoint discussion. Revised October 23 from its review card, after the organizer's October 17 approval: a new Problem 3 (a card that settles, a counter that does not), Problem 2 no longer assumes the counter approaches a place, and a fuller guide with a K–1 route; the approved PDFs are [archived](week-80/archive-before-card-fixes-2026-10-23/README.md).
