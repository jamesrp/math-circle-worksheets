# Ten geometric group theory prototypes for review

These are focused, independently reviewed prototypes for organizer review, not an adopted teaching schedule. All are unpiloted. Mathematical and digital checks do not establish physical readiness or classroom age fit; each adult guide names the material rehearsal and prerequisite limits.

The shared packets use honest Grades 3–5 or 4–5 entry bands rather than forced younger versions. Begin with selected concrete investigations; later proofs and generalizations are readiness-dependent. Every guide opens with the actual mathematical facts, assumptions and intended discoveries.

All ten complete pairs and portable source packages are available below. Combined review copies will be linked here after they are published; the standalone packets are ready to review now.

## Review and reproduction

Print single-sided US Letter at 100% / Actual Size. Each standalone source ZIP includes authored student/adult TeX, portable builders, mathematical checkers and source notes. It has been extracted into a clean directory and rebuilt using packaged inputs only; text, dimensions and every page raster match the delivered PDFs in the production environment. Different TeX/font versions may change appearance.

The [research and review record](../plans/ggt-prototypes-66-75/README.md) preserves precise primary-source lineage, independent mathematical proofs/checkers, review decisions and final artifact hashes. No whole reference books, copied source pages or borrowed workflow style examples appear in the new packages. Established mathematics is adapted into newly written investigations; novel theorems are not claimed.

Several models intentionally have limits: finite drawings are only windows into infinite roads; point shields have no radius; bounce words exclude corner hits; the area robot uses integer matrix-entry height; least stretch is a Lipschitz comparison; the elevator board is not a full Cayley graph; and the stationary paper cylinder models strand routing rather than a literal deforming sleeve. The guides retain these distinctions.
