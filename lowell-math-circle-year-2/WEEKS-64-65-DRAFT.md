## Week 64 routes

Regular-octagon edge transfers and complete horizontal trips lead to translated copies, corner identifications and three turns around the exceptional point. A separate three-square L surface supports exact path experiments, repeated square states and a finite-permutation explanation.

The regular octagon and three-square L have different flat metrics on genus-two surfaces. The L is not a dissection of the regular octagon. The packet does not present a hyperbolic tessellation or infer density from a finite drawing.

Print single-sided on US Letter at 100% / Actual Size. Essential corner cutouts are on student page 5. Start with selected pages; the guide gives prerequisites, the common launch, materials, full solutions and flexible return routes.

## Week 65 routes

The hyperbolic {8,4} tiling has four right-angled octagons at each junction. Walk one-room and two-room boundaries, compare local turns with ordinary squares, investigate complete roads that never meet, then find room-crossing routes with a shared destination. The loose record sheet can remain beside the room board.

Use a slim arrow counter about 10 mm wide, another counter and pencils. Print at Actual Size and rehearse the actual arrow on the diagrams before use. Digital clearance checks do not replace a physical rehearsal.
