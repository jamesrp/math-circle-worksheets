## Choose by prerequisites

- **98:** count small sets, recognize side-neighbors, retain the growth rule and reset a board. Later work adds exposed-edge counting, a lower-bound proof, and the difference between a smallest start and an indispensable start.
- **99:** count piles and counter-steps while keeping a fixed target visible. Later work uses unavoidable crossings, equal-center counterexamples and a star network. An adult may read or record; the child should retain the mathematical choices.
- **100:** compare labels, track nested memberships and three pairwise relations under a new distance rule. The optional number route needs parity and remainders. The object route is complete without it.

Grade labels are approximate. No K–1 packet is claimed; use an established younger-table activity when needed. Guides give a brief common launch, materials, realistic preparation, a first-visit route and later returns. Completing every page in one hour is not the goal.

## Printing and verification

Print US Letter, single-sided, at Actual Size. Week 99's last student page in each band is landscape; Week 100's student packet is landscape. Keep guides separate. Read each guide's reusable workmat and cutting-copy instructions before preparing counters or cards.

All final pages received independent visual and mathematical review. The source ZIPs contain owned TeX, builders, executable checkers, source notes and reference PDFs. Clean-extracted packages rebuild all eight PDFs byte-for-byte. See the [public verification summary and hashes](../plans/final-topics-98-100/production/README.md); detailed review reports are retained separately and are not included. Digital checks do not establish physical readiness.

The [research and original plans](../plans/final-topics-98-100/README.md) record primary sources, overlap boundaries and checked examples. The older combined print sets were not changed and do not contain Weeks 98–100. Breadth stops at 100; further work can deepen, connect, rehearse and revisit existing investigations.
