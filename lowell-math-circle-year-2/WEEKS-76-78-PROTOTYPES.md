## Read and rebuild

Print single-sided US Letter at 100% / Actual Size. The guide supplies the short common demonstration, preparation, a manageable first route, exact solutions and hints. Later proofs are readiness-dependent; completing every page is not the goal.

For every row, the actual portable ZIP has been extracted and rebuilt without repository inputs. Text, page dimensions and every page raster match the delivered PDFs in the production environment. Different TeX/font versions may affect appearance. The sources contain original student/adult LaTeX, standard-library builders, independent mathematical checkers and provenance. No whole reference book or paper, borrowed workflow style excerpt, external artwork or cached font is bundled.

The [research and review record](../plans/frontier-prototypes-76-78/README.md) distinguishes research-stage examples, student revisions, adult-guide checks and final reconstruction evidence. These are new teaching presentations of established mathematics, not newly discovered theorems.

## What is new

- Substitution strips recover hidden pair boundaries and distinguish whole generations from crops, ending with an eventual-nonperiodicity argument.
- Persistent holes preserve exact saved loops, expose equal counts with different survival histories, and test when two distinct loops become equivalent.
- Three-armed lines use min-plus corner loci, complete ordinary intersections including shared rays, and inverse constructions from target points.

The finished batch contains 13 student pages and 12 adult-guide pages. No combined book is needed to print or rebuild a single activity.
