# Sources and mathematical scope

The finite and affine instances, drawings, tasks and solutions are newly authored. The exact calculations and bounded-error bounds are independently derived, not copied exercises.

Inspected NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook sources:

- Section 2.3.6.1, [Models for instrument calibration](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/section3/mpc361.htm): linear calibration model `Y=a+bX+epsilon`, known references, and limits of identifying a model from plotted data.
- Section 2.3.6.3, [Assumptions for instrument calibration](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/section3/mpc363.htm): reference errors and separation of standards.
- Section 2.4.5, [Analysis of bias](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/section4/mpc45.htm): systematic error and calibration to a reference standard.

The upper task uses independently derived deterministic bounded errors, not NIST's normal-error regression method. It makes no confidence-level claim. The repeated-measurement argument assumes no independent centered randomness. One reference can identify a finite-prior machine in some cases, while one reference never identifies unrestricted positive slope plus arbitrary shift. The final design task minimizes worst-case slope uncertainty, not every realized positive-slope range.
