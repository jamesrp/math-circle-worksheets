## prompt
A machine gives 7, 11, and 15 for lengths 2, 4, and 6. Its rule stays fixed, but nobody promises stretch-and-shift. Make two complete report tables that fit these reports and disagree at length 8. Could any number of matching tests below length 8 force its report at 8?
