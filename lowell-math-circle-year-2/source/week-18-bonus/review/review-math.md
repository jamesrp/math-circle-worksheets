## Located mathematical issue

**Grades 2–5, page 4, Problem 6.** Exact text: “Find the fewest different counters you can flip while still passing every check.” The literal answer is **zero**, because the starting mat already passes every check. This is incompatible with the intended nonempty invisible-damage investigation and “Explain why fewer flips cannot work.” Exhaustive enumeration of all 65,536 subsets of the 16 cells finds one empty pattern, no nonempty invisible patterns of sizes 1–3, and 36 of size 4. Smallest robust fix: require at least one chosen counter and flip each chosen counter **once**. The intended pattern model chooses distinct positions and toggles each once; if repeated physical flips are permitted, one counter flipped twice restores the mat and uses only one distinct counter. The explicit once-per-chosen-counter clause closes that alternative reading.
