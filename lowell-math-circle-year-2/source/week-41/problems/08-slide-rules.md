## text @16,25 w=180 size=11
Journeys may move through the squares and cross themselves. Keep the start and finish of the whole journey at H, and keep its starting copy fixed. Add or erase two consecutive steps along the same segment in opposite directions. Within a journey, replace two adjacent sides of a square of cell centers by its other two sides, in either direction and any rotation.
