## key
Under the new three-swap rule, four different histories produce ABC: (1,2,3), (1,3,2), (2,1,3), and (3,2,1). For example, the first uses three self-swaps; the second swaps the last two twice; the third swaps the first two twice; the fourth swaps the first and last twice. These are exact collisions in the rule, not repeated observations of a single story. Other valid collisions are acceptable.
