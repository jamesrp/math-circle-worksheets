## prompt @19
A friend says six random shuffles must show all six rows once. Can you make a possible six-shuffle story that settles this?

## figure
lines(22, 2)

## key
Use (1,2) for each of six random shuffles. From ABC that produces ABC six times, and every one of those ticket draws is legal. More generally repeating any one valid story refutes the claim. Uniform probability does not force a perfectly representative batch of six results.
