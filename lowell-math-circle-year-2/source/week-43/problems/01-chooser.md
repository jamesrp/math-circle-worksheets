## text @0 size=13 [K-1]
Mix the three picture cards in a cup. Draw the first without looking and keep it out. Draw the second from the two left. Put the last card at the end. Return all cards for a new row.

## text @0 size=13
Mix the three picture cards in a cup. Each card has the same chance. Draw the first without looking and keep it out. Draw the second from the two left. Put the last card at the end. Return all cards for a new row.
