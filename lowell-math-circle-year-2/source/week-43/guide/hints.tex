For completeness: ``Fix the first card; what can happen to the last two?'' For swaps: ``Which slot contains the card you want first?'' For the wrong rule: ``How many equally likely stories would each row need?'' For identical histories from different starts: ``Can you undo each swap in reverse order?'' To decide fairness: ``Use every possible choice story and its final row.'' A short run of random shuffles cannot settle the question.
