# Week 20 shared encore student pages

Build with `bash build.sh [output-directory]`; set `PDFLATEX=/path/to/pdflatex` if needed. Requires TikZ. PDF and TeX intermediates default to `..`. The source needs no other project files.

Print US Letter at 100%. Main graph circles and squares are 30 mm wide. The worked convention figures are compact pencil demonstrations, not counter mats. Supply a second mat or plain paper for simultaneous BEFORE/AFTER states, fair-choice slips, and fraction cards. Handling is untested; this revised student packet is unpiloted.

Prepare two recoverable copies (BEFORE and AFTER) of every updating graph, including the two-circle and four-cycle boards for Problems 3–4. Supply exact number/fraction cards; whole cube stacks alone do not represent the later ticks. Terminal-score samples estimate expectations; the separate answer notes give exact explanations.
