## Independently authored classroom content

The switch cards, boards, printed target words, comparisons, state records and page designs are authored for this packet. A complete depth-d downward binary tree, persistent cards, and one completed counter at a time are essential to the tree theorem. For a path 0,…,m, m is the edge count; 1≤s<m is a fixed entry, and both endpoints absorb. Position plus the number of next-right cards is invariant during a walk. It proves exact s-right-per-m consecutive outputs from any initial state, without burn-in, while full states may have a transient.

The explicit counterexample is m=4, s=1, initial next-arrow word LLR: outputs 0,0,4,0 leave RLL. It demonstrates failure of immediate state return, not perpetual changes at block boundaries. Trials and exhaustive finite checks support examples; general proofs are stated separately in the facilitator guide.
