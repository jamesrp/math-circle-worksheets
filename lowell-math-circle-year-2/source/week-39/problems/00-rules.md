## text @16,25 w=180 size=11
Move a pawn on the roads. Keep each journey as ordered step tiles. Two adjacent steps along the same road in opposite directions are an out-and-back pair. Remove only out-and-back pairs, and replay the other steps in order. The map, start, and finish stay fixed.
