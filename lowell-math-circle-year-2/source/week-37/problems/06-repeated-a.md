## prompt [K-1]
Put A, A, B and C on each model. Can your partner make a pair that you cannot turn to match?

## prompt [2-3]
Use A, A, B and C on each model. Find out whether your partner can make a pair that will not match by turning.

No impossible pair exists with exactly A, A, B, C on congruent regular models. Every assignment belongs to one rotational class. Children can demonstrate examples; page 5 supplies the all-cases reason.

## key [2-3]
