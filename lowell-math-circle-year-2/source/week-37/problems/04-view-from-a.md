## prompt
Look straight from A toward the center of the opposite face, with A nearest your eye. Use this view of both models from Problem 2 to explain whether any turn can match them.

## figure
view_from_a()

## key
In the printed view from A, the original opposite face reads B, C, D counterclockwise, up to choice of starting place. The mirror reads B, D, C counterclockwise. A planar rotation of the view only changes the starting place, not this orientation. Use A nearest the eye on both; viewing one from the opposite side reverses the comparison and can create a false match.
