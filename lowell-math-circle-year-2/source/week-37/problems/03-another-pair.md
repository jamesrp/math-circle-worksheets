## prompt @18
Keep the pair from Problem 2. Put A, B, C and D on another model in any way you like. Which of the two does it match? Can you make one that matches neither?

## key
The new model matches exactly one of the two, so no model matches neither: four distinct labels make two classes, and the Problem 2 pair has one model in each. To check, put the new model's A on each A and try the three turns.
