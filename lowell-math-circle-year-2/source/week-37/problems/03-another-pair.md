Make another pair with A, B, C and D. Can you match the two models by turning?

## key
Answers depend on the pair built. Four distinct labels produce two classes: same-class pairs match, opposite-class pairs do not. The examples in K-1 Problem 5 supply both outcomes. Do not automatically call every newly made pair a mirror pair.
