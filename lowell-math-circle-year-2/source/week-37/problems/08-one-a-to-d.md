## prompt @18
Change one A on each model to D. Can you choose the changes to make a matching pair? Can you choose them to make a pair that will not match?

## key
Both outcomes are possible. In the printed pair, the first model has A at top and front left; the second has A at top and front right. Change the top A on both to D: the new pair does not match. Change the top A on the first and the front-right A on the second: it matches. Changing both side As gives a nonmatch; changing first-side and second-top gives a match.
