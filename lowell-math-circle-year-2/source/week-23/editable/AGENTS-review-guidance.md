# Review guidance addendum

Approved October 3, 2026. Use these paragraphs as additions to the project guidance, alongside any existing AGENTS.md. This addendum does not replace an existing AGENTS.md.

Begin every facilitator guide with a short mathematical overview that can be understood without reading the individual solutions. State the main facts, invariants, and theorems precisely, including their assumptions and any important limits. Explain what the activity is meant to help children discover, and briefly map those facts to the grade bands. Distinguish experimenting with a pattern, formulating a conjecture, and explaining why it holds. Put longer proofs and problem-by-problem solutions afterward.

When a task introduces an unfamiliar mathematical object, representation, or procedure, give a small, explicit visual example before asking children to use it. Show what a valid object looks like; for a conversion or traversal, show the input, the process, and the resulting representation with matching labels. Choose examples that clarify the task while leaving its main discovery open. Do not add examples mechanically when the task is already visually clear or a simple facilitator demonstration suffices. Keep diagrams and labels easy to read, recording demands light, and questions genuinely open rather than announcing the conclusion children are meant to investigate.
