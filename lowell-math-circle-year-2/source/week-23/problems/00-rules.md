## text @0 size=14 [K-1]
Cards move left to right. At each bar, look at the two cards at its ends: the one with fewer dots goes up. Equal cards stay. The bars stay the same for every start. The machine sorts when the fewest dots finish at the top.

## text @0 size=12.5 [2-3, 4-5]
Move from left to right. At each bar, compare only the two lanes with black dots: the smaller value goes to the upper lane and the larger to the lower lane. Ties may stay put. Keep every bar fixed for every start; move cards only at bars. A machine sorts when the final values never get smaller from lane 1 down.
