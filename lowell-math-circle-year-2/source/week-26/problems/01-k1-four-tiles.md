## prompt @66
Make every different shape you can with four tiles. Draw each shape and count its boundary sides.

## figure
k1_four_tile_grids()

## key
\emph{Print guide, p. 4.} \textbf{Exact answer:} five shapes up to turns and flips. The square has 8 exposed sides; the other four have 10. The sixth blank recording grid is not a request for a sixth shape.

For the counts, the square has four shared sides, giving $16 - 8 = 8$. Each other shape has three shared sides, giving $16 - 6 = 10$. Let children check with markers before introducing the subtraction shorthand.
