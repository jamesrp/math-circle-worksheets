## Located convention ambiguity

Grades K–5, page 2, Problem 2/shared rule: “Read a stack from top to bottom.” and “Keep the original front marked. ‘Toward the front’ and ‘toward the back’ refer to that original front at each join.” A completed stack can be turned over: its top-to-bottom word reverses while its crease directions relative to the original marked front stay unchanged. For example, front/back directions that yield ABC with B's original front upward also display CBA when the whole stack is turned over. Thus the writer's intended unique mixed-direction classification needs a reference orientation; the substantive six-order existence result is unchanged. Smallest fix: “Read the stack with B's original front facing up.” The adult answer classification should explicitly use the same convention.
