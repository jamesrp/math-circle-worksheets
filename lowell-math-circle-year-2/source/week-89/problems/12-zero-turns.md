## prompt
Where should the two zeros go to make the second total largest? Where should they go to make it smallest? Keep the 1 before the 3. Start both trays at 0.

## text
\begin{center}\slips{1,3}\hspace{12mm}\slips{0,0}\end{center}
A zero input is a whole turn: add 0 to the first total, then add a copy of that first total to the second.
\answer{55mm}
