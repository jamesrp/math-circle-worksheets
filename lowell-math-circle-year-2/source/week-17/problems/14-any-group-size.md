## prompt size=15
A machine must say YES exactly when its red cards can be grouped in fives with none left over. Find the fewest circles it needs, and do the same for groups of six. A row with no red cards must give YES. For any group size of one or more, describe a machine, explain why it works for every row, and explain why fewer circles cannot work.
