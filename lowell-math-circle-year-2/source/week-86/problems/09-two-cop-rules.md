## text @0,0 w=192 size=11
Use C1 and C2. Put down both cops before R chooses. On a cop turn, each cop may stay or take one road. Then R gets one turn. Flip the tile after the whole cop turn and after R's turn. Capture is immediate. Cops may share a dot.
