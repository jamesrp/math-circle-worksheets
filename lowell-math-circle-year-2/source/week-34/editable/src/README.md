# Week 34 revised student packet

Student PDFs only. Draft, unpiloted. Since October 23, 2026 the packets are stored as problem files (in the repository `../../week.toml`, `../../figures.py` and `../../problems/`; in the ZIP `../problem-files/`). Run `bash build.sh` with Python 3.11+, pdfLaTeX with TikZ, and Poppler installed: `regenerate.py` writes the three `.tex` sources from the problem files (edit those, not the `.tex`), and `check_distinguishing.py` verifies the cycle distinguishing numbers for 3 through 10 positions. The previous builder (`build_packets.py`) is archived beside the problem files. No machine-specific TeX paths are needed by these sources.

K–1 and Grades 2–3 have five pages; Grades 4–5 has six, the sixth being extra workspace (a seven-ring and a nine-ring) for Problem 5. Print US Letter at 100 percent. Position circles are 28 mm in diameter, on a 140 mm centerline ring. Each child needs counters in three fixed kinds and tracing-paper copies coloured with the counters' colours. Do not put a permanent home mark on a mat or overlay. The introductory example uses filled and hollow circles. All spots must coincide after a legal test. The little worked example is on an alternating four-ring and shows, each from the same start, a one-spot turn (no match), a two-spot turn (match) and a flip over a drawn axis (match); no successful asymmetric six-ring is printed.

The student problems intentionally leave construction methods and impossibility arguments open. Blank ring centers can hold sketches or oral explanations can be scribed on separate paper. Each large diagram is a working mat, not a small recording icon. The diagrams are original.

Source of mathematical setting: Albertson and Collins, Symmetry Breaking in Graphs (1996), https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/v3i1r18 . The supplied outline contains the elementary cycle proof; `mathematical-checks.json` records independent finite checks. Minimum number of kinds is distinct from the final grades 2–3 question about the minimum number of counters of one kind.

All 15 revised pages were freshly rendered and visually inspected with no overflow or overlap found. The independent coordinate-based checker confirms the finite distinguishing counts for rings of size 3 through 10, both one-counter square-edit outcomes, and distinct asymmetric eight-rings. Physical overlays and classroom timing have not been tested.
