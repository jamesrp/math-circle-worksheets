## text @0,0.25 w=18.4 [K-1]
Fill every spot with a counter. Copy the pattern on tracing paper with a dot of each counter's color. One partner builds. The other keeps the counters still and turns or flips (turns over) the copy to a new place. If every dot sits on its own color, that is a match. Putting the copy back where it started, or turning it all the way around, does not count.

## text @0,0.25 w=18.4
Fill every spot; neighbors may be the same kind. Copy a pattern on tracing paper with a dot of each counter's color. One partner builds. The other keeps the original still and turns or flips (turns over) the copy to a new place. If every dot sits on its own color, that is a match. Putting the copy back where it started, or turning it all the way around, does not count. A flip may be over any line through the middle.
