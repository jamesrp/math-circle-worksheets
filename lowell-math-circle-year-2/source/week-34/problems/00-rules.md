## text @0,0.25 w=18.4 [K-1]
Fill every spot with a counter. Copy the pattern on tracing paper with a dot of each counter's color. One partner builds. The other keeps the counters still and turns or flips (turns over) the copy to make every dot sit on its own color. A whole turn does not count.

## text @0,0.25 w=18.4
Fill every spot; neighbors may be the same kind. Only counter kinds and spots count. Copy a pattern on tracing paper with a dot of each counter's color. One partner builds. The other keeps the original still and turns or flips (turns over) the copy to make every dot sit on its own color. A whole turn does not count.
