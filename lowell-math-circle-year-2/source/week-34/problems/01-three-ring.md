## prompt @6.2 [K-1]
Make different patterns on this ring with two kinds of counter. Let your partner find turns or flips that match.

## prompt @6.2 [2-3]
Find how few kinds of counter can stop every turn and flip on this ring. Use up to three kinds; neighbors may match.

## prompt @6.2 [4-5]
Find the fewest kinds of counter that stop every turn and flip on the three-ring. Neighbors may match. Can you rule out every pattern with fewer kinds?
