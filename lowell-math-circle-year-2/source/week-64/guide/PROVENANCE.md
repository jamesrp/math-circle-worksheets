# Authorship and mathematical sources

Prepared October 5, 2026.

The guide's wording, solutions, adult prompts, timing suggestions and TikZ figure are newly authored. No source page, figure, book problem, downloaded reference or borrowed writing exemplar is included. The work uses established mathematics and does not claim novel theorems or classroom validation.

Public sources consulted:

1. Alex Wright, *From Rational Billiards to Dynamics on Moduli Spaces*, pp. 3–6; p. 10 for advanced dynamics. Translation gluing, regular octagon, single cone point of angle 6π, genus two.
   https://public.websites.umich.edu/~alexmw/BilliardsToModuli.pdf
2. The surface-dynamics authors, official *Square-tiled Surfaces* documentation, First steps and Topological invariants. Right permutation (1,2) and up permutation (1,3) confirm the model and its genus-two single-singularity stratum.
   https://flatsurf.github.io/surface-dynamics/examples/square_tiled_surfaces.html
3. Giovanni Forni and Carlos Matheus, *Introduction to Teichmüller Theory and Its Applications to Dynamics of Interval Exchange Transformations, Flows on Surfaces and Billiards*, pp. 17–18, Figure 4, and p. 78. Three-square L, translation structure and horizontal cylinders.
   https://www.math.uchicago.edu/~masur/gm.pdf
4. Jeff Erickson, *Surface Homotopy*, Universal Cover. Hyperbolic genus-two octagon context, used only to distinguish metrics.
   https://jeffe.cs.illinois.edu/teaching/comptop/2020/notes/21-surface-homotopy.html#universal-cover

The exact octagon coordinates, horizontal segment formulas, balanced three-copy development, prescribed L starts and periods, endpoint chains and elementary finite-permutation proof are worked out in the guide. The standard-library script independently checks their algebraic and finite combinatorial claims.

The octagon and L have the same genus and cone-angle pattern but different flat metrics. The L is not a dissection of the regular octagon. No rational/irrational slope classification for the regular octagon or ergodicity inference from a finite drawing is claimed.

The facilitation suggestions are proposals. Timing and material handling have not been physically pretested or classroom-piloted. Mathematical and render checks do not remove that limitation.
