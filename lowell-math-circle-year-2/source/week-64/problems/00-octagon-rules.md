## text @0,.43 w=7.35 size=12.5
Slide same-letter edges together with their small arrows pointing the same way. At an edge, move to the same place on the other same-letter edge (match the numbered ticks) and keep the same travel direction. Stop at a corner. A trip closes when its starting point and direction both return. \par
One partner draws. The other checks each transfer with tracing paper and holds a separate arrow in the travel direction. Swap roles after each trip.
