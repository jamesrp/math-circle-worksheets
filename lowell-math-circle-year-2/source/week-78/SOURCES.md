# Source lineage

- David Speyer and Bernd Sturmfels, *Tropical Mathematics* (2004), section 1, printed pages 1–2, and section 3, printed pages 6–8: min-plus operations, the repeated-minimum definition, tropical lines and generic intersection/interpolation. https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Speyer.pdf
- Ayush Kumar Tewari, *Point-Line Geometry in the Tropical Plane* (2020 preprint), sections 3–4, printed pages 3 and 5–8: point-line incidence, duality and exceptional ray configurations. https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.04425 . Its max-plus convention has the opposite ray orientation; the present packet consistently uses min-plus.

The exact case classification, rectangle proof, reverse-junction construction, numerical puzzles, diagrams and code are independently derived for this prototype. No claim is made that these are exercises reproduced from either source. The degree-one mathematics supplies a real connection to tropical algebraic geometry and incidence research; it does not establish higher-degree intersection formulas.

The sources supply mathematical lineage, not evidence of measured classroom success or permission to redistribute whole papers. No source PDF, external illustration, borrowed workflow exemplar or external font file is packaged.
