# Week 5: How much information determines a city?

Permutations, Latin-square completion, uniqueness, minimal versus minimum clue sets, and Latin trades.

- [K–1](../../week-05/week-05-k-1.pdf) — 2 pages.
- [Grades 2–3](../../week-05/week-05-grades-2-3.pdf) — 2 pages.
- [Grades 4–5](../../week-05/week-05-grades-4-5.pdf) — 3 pages.
- [Extra grades 6–7](../../week-05/week-05-extra-grades-6-7.pdf) — 1 page.
- [Facilitator and solutions](../../week-05/week-05-facilitator.pdf) — 4 pages.

Print US Letter, single-sided, 100%. Give one page at a time: the packets are investigations, not a completion quota. Current roster needs three K–1, three middle, one upper packet (15 student sheets), plus the extra if needed. Children may dictate or demonstrate.

The [redesign plan](../../../plans/week-05-redesign.md) includes prerequisites, materials, staffing, flow, sources, and use history. IDs F05-K-v2/M-v2/U-v2/X-v2 are prepared, not taught.

Rebuild from the repository root: `sh lowell-math-circle-year-2/source/week-05/build.sh`. Editable LaTeX/TikZ and local common.tex are included. Requires Python 3 and pdfLaTeX with TikZ, Source Sans Pro, microtype, fancyhdr, geometry, tabularx, hyperref, amsmath. The build runs plans/verify-week-05.py and writes PDFs to `lowell-math-circle-year-2/week-05/`. Re-render after editing. [Review record](REVIEW.md).
