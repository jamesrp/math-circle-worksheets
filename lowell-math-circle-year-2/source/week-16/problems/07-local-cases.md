## prompt [2-3]
Put R, B, or Y on each dot. Find every different filling, counting turns and flips as the same. Mark the doors in each filling. Could a triangle have three doors?

## prompt [4-5]
Put R, B, or Y on each dot. Find every different filling, counting turns and flips as the same. Record the number of doors in each filling. Which fillings have exactly one door? Could a triangle have three doors?
