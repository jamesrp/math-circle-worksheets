**Kid-fit revision, November 6, 2026.** A kid-fit pilot (critic, then reviser, student pages only; see `plans/review/KID-FIT-PILOT.md` and `plans/review/kid-fit/pilot/week-16/`) revised the student pages, and James approved adopting them on November 6, 2026. Student pages (F16-K-v4, F16-23-v4, F16-45-v4): the shared rule says "Put R, B, or Y" so counters fit it; K–1 Problem 1 is reworded to work aloud ("Find all four places"), Problem 2 is a partner hiding game (one fills, the other hunts; can the filler ever win?), Problem 5 records each place found with an X, and Problem 6 asks for two zero fillings and drops its record triangles. In Grades 2–3 and 4–5, Problem 1 has children try fillings with counters and write each record on its own board; the rows problem moves to Problem 2 and carries the one-sentence door definition; Problem 3 asks for different fillings of one triangle, with the doors marked, instead of a complete catalog; the routes problem moves to Problem 4; and Grades 2–3 Problem 5 asks only about routes from bottom-side doors. The rows problem is now on the first route and the local-cases problem on the return route. The guide is now F16-G-v4: problem numbers and its page order follow the new packets (rows and local cases before routes), the K–1 keys describe the hiding game, the X record and the two zero fillings, and the preparation adds counters for the older Problem 1 (seven sets of 6 R, 6 B and 6 Y, 126 counters, or four shared pair sets; 306 in all with the K–1 sets); its route note records the revision. Answers and mathematics are unchanged. The packets built from these problem files match the approved candidate PDFs (byte-identical, and 0 differing pixels at 100 dpi after the package build).
