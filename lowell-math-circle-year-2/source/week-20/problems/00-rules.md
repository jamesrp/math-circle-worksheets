## text @0.64,9.86 [K-1]
Squares keep their stacks. A circle holds one equal share of the stacks joined to it. Make one equal pile for each joined shape, including empty shapes. Every circle must work at once. Use spare cubes to copy and share.

## text @0.64,9.86
Add the numbers joined to a circle and divide by how many numbers you added. The answer must equal the circle's number. Squares keep their numbers. Every circle must work at the same time.
