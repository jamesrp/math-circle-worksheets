## prompt
A donut board has its edges glued together: edge A on the left is glued to edge A on the right, and edge B at the top is glued to edge B at the bottom. Leave the board on the right and you come back on the left at the same height. Leave at the top and you come back at the bottom at the same place. Countries on opposite edges share a border along the glued edge. Each number is a different country. Color each donut map with as few colors as you can, and write how many colors you used.
