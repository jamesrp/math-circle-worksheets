## prompt
On your whiteboard, draw maps using only lines that run from one edge of the board to another edge, and loops that end where they started. The lines and loops may wiggle and cross each other and themselves as often as you like. Can you draw a map like this that cannot be colored with two colors? Draw the map you think is hardest to color in the box, and color it.
