## prompt
Play with a partner and your two thick pencils. One of you is the Painter and one is the Spoiler. Take turns coloring an empty square. It must not match a square next to it. The Painter goes first. If an empty square gets stuck, the Spoiler wins. If every square gets colored, the Painter wins. Who can win every time? Circle the answer.
