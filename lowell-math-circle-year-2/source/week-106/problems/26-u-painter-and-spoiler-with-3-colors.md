## prompt
Play this game with a partner and 3 colors. One of you is the Painter and the other is the Spoiler. On your turn, color any empty square with one of the 3 colors, keeping the coloring rule. A square is stuck if the squares it shares borders with already use all 3 colors. The Spoiler wins if a square gets stuck, and the Painter wins if all 9 squares get colored. Play games with each player going first. Which player can win every time? Explain how.
