A has $m$ different whole-number cards and B has $n$. Each input has at least two cards. Suppose they give exactly $m+n-1$ different totals. Must both inputs be equally spaced, with the same gap in A and B? Explain why, or find a pair that breaks the claim. Also explain why any two equally spaced inputs with the same gap give exactly $m+n-1$ totals.
