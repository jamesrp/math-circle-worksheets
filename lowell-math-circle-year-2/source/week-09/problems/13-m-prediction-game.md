## prompt
Play this game with your partner. One player draws a table on the grid, no more than 6 squares wide and 6 squares high, and marks its dot. The other player says where the ball will stop and how many times it will bounce, and then traces the path. The player who predicted scores 1 point for the right corner and 1 point for the right number of bounces. Then swap jobs. After each player has predicted three tables, the player with more points wins.
