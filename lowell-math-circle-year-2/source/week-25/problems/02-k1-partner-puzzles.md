## prompt @27
Make a four-counter picture on each left grid and write its counts. Can your partner make a different picture with those counts on the right grid?

## key
\emph{Print guide, p. 7.} \textbf{P2:} there is no single required picture. Make any four-counter picture on each left grid, enter all counts, and let the partner test whether a different picture exists. Either answer can be correct. The partner first checks the counts: a finger along each row and down each column to its circle, before the maker draws.
