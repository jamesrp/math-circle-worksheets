## key
\emph{Print guide, p. 5.} \textbf{Answer:} exactly six. The first four recording grids are on page 4; the remaining two are at the top of page 5. The row letters stay fixed.

The A counter can occupy column 1, 2, or 3. For each choice, B can use either of the two remaining columns. C must use the last column. Thus there are two pictures for each of three choices: 2 + 2 + 2 = 6.
