## What changed

- **Must (card fix 1), p. 11, Grades 2–3 Problem 4 held hints.** The old note said counting four moved counters "is not by itself a lower bound". It is one: A1, A2, B3 and B4 must each be emptied and each switch empties exactly two cells, so at least two switches are needed. The hint now offers this argument; a route that moves a counter twice only adds switches.
- **Should (fix 8), p. 1, supplies.** 24 counters per table (two pairs, each able to keep two six-counter pictures side by side; the partner tasks K–1 P5 and grades 2–3 P4–P5 need eight per pair). The never-supplied "reusable labeled grids" are dropped: children place counters on the printed grids.
- **Should (fix 2), p. 2, launch.** The old launch (A1, A3, B2; rows 2, 1, columns 1, 1, 1) has three pictures, is a printed task, and the guide then drew a second matching picture, against its own "let children encounter ambiguity first". The launch now uses A1, A2, B1 (rows 2, 1; columns 2, 1, 0), whose counts fit only that picture, and stops once a child rebuilds it.

Deferred, because they change student pages and wait for a problem-file conversion: fixes 3 (spare grids, with guide pp. 4–5 and 12–13), 4, 5, 6, 7 (a recorded switch, with guide p. 10) and 9. The coulds (10–14) are not done.
