## prompt
Put each block under its picture on machine A and do one turn. Move the row to machine B without changing its order, and do one turn there. Draw the blocks where they end up in the row marked A then B. Then start again with one turn of B followed by one turn of A, and draw the row marked B then A. Do this for both pairs of machines. For which pair are the two rows the same? Explain why.
