## text
\band{2--5}
The equator is a great circle. $N$ is the pole above it. A meridian is a great-circle semicircle from $N$ to the opposite pole.

## prompt
Keep $N$ fixed. Move $A$ and $B$ on the equator, using its shorter arc as the third side. Make triangles whose corner at $N$ is smaller than a right angle, equal to a right angle, and larger than a right angle. Draw each triangle and mark all its corners. Which changes make the triangle cover more of the ball?
