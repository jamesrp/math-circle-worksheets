# Sources and mathematical lineage

The activity sequence, diagrams, example records, guide, and software are newly authored for this collection. No worksheet or published page is reproduced. The printed repeated records are invented examples, not classroom observations or simulation output.

- Brett McClintock, *Mark-resight models*, chapter 18 in Cooch and White's *Program MARK: A Gentle Introduction*, PDF pp. 2–3 (printed 18-2–18-3), [author-hosted chapter](https://sites.warnercnr.colostate.edu/gwhite/wp-content/uploads/sites/73/2017/05/MARK_resight_help.pdf). The design researcher inspected the actual PDF: the Lincoln–Petersen setup, population closure, retained marks, representative sampling, and with/without-replacement distinctions inform the model. The packet explicitly uses sampling without replacement inside each catch. Our finite-population constructions and proofs are supplied in the guide rather than delegated to the source.
- Laura Givental, Maria Nemirovskaya and Ilya Zakharevich, *Math Circle by the Bay*, printed pp. viii–x (PDF pp. 9–11), locally authorized reference inspected by the parent researcher and reread by this family coordinator. The introductory discussion supports deep topics and connections, manipulatives, independent problem solving and flexible pacing across years. Applying those lessons to this sequence is our design judgment, not a claim of classroom validation.
- Existing collection connections inspected in actual PDFs: Week 6 (queries), Week 18 (error correction), Week 25 (tomography), Weeks 42–45 (probability and bias), Week 51 and its bonus (honest ranges and fixed-sum rectangles), and atlas AP02–AP05 (randomization, bias, coverage). This family centers on inferring an unseen population from overlapping samples, incompatible single-record estimates of one fixed population, and a model-based catch allocation. Fixed-sum rectangle reasoning returns as the proof for maximizing expected overlap, not as the family's main new result.

Under the stated uniform-subset model, the guide proves the overlap support, sharp lower population bound and absence of a finite upper bound, expected overlap, ratio-estimate limitations, and balanced-budget optimum. General proofs are supplemented by independent exact checks. Maximum expected overlap is not asserted to mean minimum estimation error under every criterion. Physical shaking does not prove uniform sampling.

Only owned teaching and build/check files are packaged. No borrowed book or source chapter, private records, workflow exemplar text, or raw agent transcript is included. Unpiloted; physical materials and procedures remain unrehearsed.
