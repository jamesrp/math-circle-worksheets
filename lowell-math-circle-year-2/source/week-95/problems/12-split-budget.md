## prompt
A bag has 24 objects. You may handle 12 objects altogether: first catch size + second catch size = 12. Choose the split with the greatest average overlap over many rounds. Explain why no other split does better.

## text
The average over all equally likely second catches is
\[
\text{average overlap}=\frac{\text{first catch size}\times\text{second catch size}}{\text{bag total}}.
\]
\work{1.7in}
