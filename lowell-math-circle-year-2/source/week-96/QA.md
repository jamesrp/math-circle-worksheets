## Mathematical verification

The independent standard-library checker exhaustively traverses legal factor splits for all printed starting values; compares sub-bag products with direct divisor lists; independently enumerates exponent patterns and multiplicative partitions; and tests every integer below every claimed minimum. It includes the empty bag and repeated-prime cases. The guide separately proves unique factorization via Euclid's lemma, the divisor/sub-bag bijection, the product count, and minimality across all primes. Finite computation is not represented as a proof of universal statements.
