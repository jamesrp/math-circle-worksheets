# Sources and provenance

The student problems, figures and guide are newly authored for this library. Mathematical facts are classical; no new research result is claimed. References are mathematical and pedagogical evidence, not claims that the source tested these particular worksheets. No whole reference book, downloaded paper or borrowed workflow excerpt is included in this portable package.

## Mathematical sources

Keith Conrad, *Unique Factorization in Z and F[T]*, Theorems 1.1, 2.1, 2.3 and Lemma 2.2, PDF pp. 1–3: existence and uniqueness of prime factorization, and the prime-divides-a-product lemma. Read on October 17, 2026.
https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/ugradnumthy/uniquefactnZF%5BT%5D.pdf

The sub-bag/divisor bijection, product formula for the number of divisors and finite minimum-number arguments are derived independently from this theorem. Labels on repeated prime tokens do not distinguish copies; selecting zero tokens represents 1.
