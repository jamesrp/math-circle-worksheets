# Verification and limits

Fresh writer, adversarial critic, independent mathematical checker and reviser stages were completed. The adult guide was separately authored and the final student/guide pair independently reviewed. Final changed examples were checked again. Every PDF page was rendered and inspected; the current source archive was extracted and rebuilt without repository inputs, comparing all page images, text, page counts and dimensions.

- week-77-students.pdf: 5 pages; SHA-256 e27735de07fee5b65d5f8ea1be06e260f4724a12ca039e10bd8b8d704a4d4787
- week-77-facilitator.pdf: 4 pages; SHA-256 86d65e917d1b11f04e107921a26189a68cc777a03b53f65c9d9d1021fd6775fc

The mathematical checks distinguish finite computation from general proofs. Digital reconstruction does not validate physical tile/card handling, printing/tracing, timing, staffing or classroom age fit. Physical rehearsal and classroom piloting are unperformed.

Production used the installed TeX packages with an externally initialized runtime format because the cloud image lacks its normal TeX database/format setup. No generated runtime input is included or needed on a normal TeX installation.
