# Source lineage

Herbert Edelsbrunner, David Letscher and Afra Zomorodian, *Topological Persistence and Simplification* (2002), PDF pages 2–3, section 2, for simplicial complexes, mod-2 chains/boundaries and growing filters; PDF pages 4–6, section 3, for persistence and inclusion-induced maps. https://pub.ista.ac.at/~edels/Papers/2002-J-04-TopologicalPersistence.pdf

The square, vertex-touching triangles and four-triangle fan; their schedules and saved-loop challenges; the physical token convention; diagrams; solutions and code are independently designed finite teaching models. They are not reproduced source exercises. Their exact mathematics is validated through literal boundary cancellation and inclusion behavior, without importing the paper's three-dimensional alpha-complex machinery or general pairing algorithm as child-facing content.

The packet's deeper connection is persistence of classes across a growing family: snapshot hole counts can agree while the survival maps differ, and distinct edge loops can become equivalent after fillings. The new fan task makes that equivalence consequential through reversible ordering experiments on a reset board.

No complete paper, copied source figure, borrowed workflow exemplar, cached font or external data is packaged. Source attribution records mathematical lineage; it is not evidence of classroom effectiveness or permission to redistribute the source paper. General persistence stability and metric point-cloud inference were considered in research but are not claims of this final prototype.
