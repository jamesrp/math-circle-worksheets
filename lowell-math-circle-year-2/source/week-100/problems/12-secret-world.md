## prompt
Use cards A through F and labels 2, 4, 8. One player builds a world in secret. The other asks for distances between pairs until they can draw a world with the same distances. Try to use as few questions as possible. Then the builder shows the secret world: if any distance in the drawing is different, the round is lost. Trade places.
