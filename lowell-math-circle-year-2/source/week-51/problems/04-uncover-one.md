## prompt @190
A different pair has A from 4 to 6 and B from 3 to 4. You may learn one exact length. Which strip would you uncover to leave the narrowest guaranteed range for the join?

## key
Uncover \textbf{A}. Knowing A=a leaves [a+3,a+4], width 1. Knowing B=b leaves [b+4,b+6], width 2. Revealing the strip with the wider original range removes more uncertainty; the exact revealed value shifts the remaining range but does not change its width.
