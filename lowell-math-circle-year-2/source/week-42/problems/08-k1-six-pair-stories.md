## prompt @0
Choose pairs and draw six-pair stories using your rule for each target. Can six random pairs force a shape to appear?

## figure
k1_stories()

## key
Examples of six-pair stories: \textbf{no shapes:} RR, RR, RR, RR, RR, RR. \textbf{Exactly one square, no circles:} BR, RR, RR, RR, RR, RR. \textbf{Exactly three circles, no squares:} RB, RB, RB, RR, RR, RR. Any ordering with the required outputs works. Six random pairs cannot force a shape: the first story has positive probability.
