## prompt @13.8
Can your rule promise at least one shape within six pairs?

## figure
lines(15.2,2)

## key
No. RR repeated six times is a legal no-shape story. With replacement and the original bag its chance is (9/16)$^{6}$, which is positive. Children need only exhibit the legal story, not compute its chance. Hint: ask for a six-pair story that settles it.
