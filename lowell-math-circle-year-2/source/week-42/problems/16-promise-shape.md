## prompt @12
Can your rule promise at least one shape within six pairs? Make a six-pair story that settles your answer.

## figure
box_only(0,14.4,18,3.5)

## key
No. RR repeated six times is a legal no-shape story. With replacement and the original bag its chance is (9/16)$^{6}$, which is positive. Children need only exhibit the legal story, not compute its chance.
