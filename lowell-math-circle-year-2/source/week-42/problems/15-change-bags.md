## key
No. Use the printed first bag \textbf{three red, one blue} and second bag \textbf{one red, three blue}. The exact counts are RR 3, RB 9, BR 1, BB 3 among 16 equally likely cross-bag identity pairs. For the key rule, circle has 9 ways and square 1; conditional on a shape, their chances are 9/10 and 1/10. Reversal is no longer an equally likely outcome of this same experiment.
