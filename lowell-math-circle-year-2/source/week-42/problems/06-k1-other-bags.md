## prompt @0
Keep your rule for these bags. Which can give each shape, and do the shapes have the same chance?

## figure
k1_other_bags()

## key
One red/three blue gives each shape in 3 of 16 marked pairs (the R, B1, B2, B3 pictures on page 4). Two red/two blue gives each shape in 4 of 16 (the R1, R2, B1, B2 pictures). In both bags the shapes have equal positive chances. Four red or four blue gives no shape at all, so neither can give each shape.
