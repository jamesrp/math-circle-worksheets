## prompt @0
Keep your rule for these bags. Which can give each shape, and do the shapes have the same chance?

## figure
k1_other_bags()

## key
One red/three blue gives each shape in 3 of 16 marked pairs. Two red/two blue gives each shape in 4 of 16. In both bags the shapes have equal positive chances. Four red or four blue gives no shape at all, so neither can give each shape.
