## prompt @12
You want shapes to come out often. Would you choose the three-red bag or the two-red bag? Give an exact reason.

## figure
bags([(1,15,'RRRB',''),(10,15,'RRBB','')],(19,4))

## key
The two-red bag produces a shape in 8 of 16 pairs; the three-red bag in 6 of 16. Choose the two-red bag. This improves how often the chooser responds, while both bags give fair shapes when they respond.
