## prompt @15
You want shapes to come out often. Would you choose the three-red bag or the two-red bag? Give an exact reason.

## figure
bags([(1,16.8,'RRRB',''),(10,16.8,'RRBB','')],(19.6,4))

## key
The two-red bag produces a shape in 8 of 16 pairs; the three-red bag in 6 of 16. Choose the two-red bag. Both bags are fair when a shape comes out.
