## prompt @5
Choose draws yourself to make every different color pair. Which pairs disappear if you take the blue counter out of the bag?

## figure
k1_pair_boxes()

## key
All four color pairs are RR, RB, BR, BB. Replacement makes BB possible even with only one blue counter. Taking the blue counter out leaves only RR: RB, BR, and BB disappear. A child may choose these draws deliberately to show possibility.
