## prompt @17.3
Can any fair rule give a shape on every pair from the original three-red, one-blue bag? Explain your answer.

## key
No color-only deterministic rule can be both fair and always produce a shape. All nine RR labeled pairs receive the same shape, already more than half of the 16 outcomes. The other shape cannot catch up even if it receives all seven remaining outcomes.
