## prompt @18
Choose a four-counter bag that makes shapes come out as often as possible. Could any four-counter bag give a shape on every pair?

## figure
k1_two_boxes()

## key
Choose two red and two blue. For red counts 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, the numbers of mixed labeled pairs are 0, 6, 8, 6, 0 out of 16. Hence 2-and-2 is optimal. No four-counter bag gives a shape on every pair under this rule: repeating the identity of the first counter is always possible and makes a same-color pair.
