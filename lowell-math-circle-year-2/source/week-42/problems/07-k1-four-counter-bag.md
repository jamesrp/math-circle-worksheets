## key
Choose two red and two blue: the R1, R2, B1, B2 pictures on page 4 give a shape in 8 of 16. For red counts 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, the numbers of mixed labeled pairs are 0, 6, 8, 6, 0 out of 16. Hence 2-and-2 is optimal. No four-counter bag gives a shape on every pair under this rule: repeating the identity of the first counter is always possible and makes a same-color pair. A changed rule could: with two red and two blue, first color red gives a square and blue a circle is fair and never skips (8 and 8 of 16). That is why the problem says to keep your rule.
