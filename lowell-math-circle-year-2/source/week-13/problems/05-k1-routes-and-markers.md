## prompt
Fit the most routes on each board. Then stop all travel with the same number of markers.

## figure
k1_routes_and_markers()

## key
Upper board below: 2 routes, for example s-A-D-E-t and s-B-D-F-t; close DE and DF. Lower three-branch board: 3 routes, s-A-t, s-B-t and s-C-t; close sA, sB and sC. Every route uses one selected outgoing Start arrow.

## hints
Hint: a shared dot may have several incoming arrows but fewer outgoing arrows.
