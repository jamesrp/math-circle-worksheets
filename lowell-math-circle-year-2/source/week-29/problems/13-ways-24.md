## prompt @14
Find every way to build 24 with 3-rods and 4-rods. How can you change one way into another?

## figure
draw(('ruled', 17, 6))

## key
\emph{Print guide, p. 3.} All ways for 24 are (8,0),(4,3),(0,6). Replace four 3-rods by three 4-rods, or reverse that replacement. Equality of two builds gives $3\Delta x=-4\Delta y$, forcing changes to be multiples of this exchange.
