## prompt @15
Someone says every pair of rod lengths eventually has no more gaps. Test 2 and 4, then 4 and 7. Is the claim true?

## figure
draw(('ruled', 18, 5))

## key
\emph{Print guide, p. 3.} The claim is false: 2/4 misses every odd length. The pair 4/7 does eventually have no gaps, as P5 proves. One counterexample is enough to refute ``every pair.''
