## key [K-1]
\emph{Print guide, p. 2.} For 12: four 3-rods or three 4-rods. For 15: five 3-rods, or one 3-rod and three 4-rods. These are all: after choosing the number of 4-rods, the remainder must be a multiple of 3. The third strip under each target stays empty.

## key [2-3]
\emph{Print guide, p. 3.} Writing (number of 3-rods, number of 4-rods): 12 has (4,0),(0,3); 16 has (4,1),(0,4); 24 has (8,0),(4,3),(0,6). The number of 4-rods has one required remainder modulo 3, so stepping by three 4-rods lists every possibility.
