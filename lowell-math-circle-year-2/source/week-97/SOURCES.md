# Sources and provenance

The student problems, figures and guide are newly authored for this library. Mathematical facts are classical; no new research result is claimed. References are mathematical and pedagogical evidence, not claims that the source tested these particular worksheets. No whole reference book, downloaded paper or borrowed workflow excerpt is included in this portable package.

## Mathematical sources

Keith Conrad, *Modular Arithmetic*, §3, PDF pp. 3–4: multiplication is independent of remainder representative; equal squares can have more than opposite roots for composite moduli. Read on October 17, 2026.
https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/ugradnumthy/modarith.pdf

Keith Conrad, *Quadratic Residue Patterns Modulo a Prime*, introduction, PDF p. 1: half the nonzero residues of an odd prime are squares; zero is excluded from the source's quadratic residue/nonresidue convention. Our phrase "square remainders" includes zero and says so.
https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/ugradnumthy/QuadraticResiduePatterns.pdf

Prime root-pairing and product-class arguments are derived in the adult guide; finite inventories are independently checked. No quadratic reciprocity or large-prime distribution result is inferred from the small examples.
