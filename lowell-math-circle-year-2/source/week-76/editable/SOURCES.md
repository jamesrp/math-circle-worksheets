# Source lineage

The wording, teaching instances, diagrams, verification code and adult presentation in this package are newly authored around established Thue–Morse mathematics. No novel discovery of the sequence or its theorems is claimed.

- Richard G. Swan, *The Morse Sequence*, author-hosted exposition, page 2, Lemma 2.2: local factors and pairing parity. https://www.math.uchicago.edu/~swan/expo/Morse.pdf
- Carl D. Offner, *Repetitions of Words and the Thue-Morse sequence*, section 3, pages 2–3: even/odd identities and local repetition restrictions. https://www.cs.umb.edu/~offner/files/thue.pdf
- Jean-Paul Allouche and Jeffrey Shallit, *The ubiquitous Prouhet-Thue-Morse sequence*, sections 2–4, especially Propositions 3–4: substitution fixed point, recurrence and non-eventual-periodicity. https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~shallit/Papers/ubiq15.pdf

The period-halving presentation, crop choices and deceptive repeating-strip comparison were independently arranged for this prototype. Source references establish mathematical lineage, not measured classroom effectiveness or permission to republish entire papers. No paper, borrowed figure, workflow exemplar or external font file is included.

Broader research context: Smith, Myers, Kaplan and Goodman-Strauss, *An aperiodic monotile*, https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~csk/hat/ . Hierarchical substitution links this strip investigation to aperiodic order, but the packet does not reproduce hat geometry or prove the hat theorem.
