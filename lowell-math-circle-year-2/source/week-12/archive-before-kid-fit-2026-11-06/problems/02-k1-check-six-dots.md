## prompt
Check your six-dot collection. Show how you know that no pairing is missing.

## figure
k1_mat_and_records(6)

## key
\emph{Print guide, p. 3.} Check the P1 collection for repeats or omissions. Exactly five pairings: 12|34|56, 12|36|45, 14|23|56, 16|23|45, 16|25|34. To establish completeness, 1 can meet only 2, 4 or 6; these give 2, 1 and 2 completions. A chord leaves an even number of dots on each side.
