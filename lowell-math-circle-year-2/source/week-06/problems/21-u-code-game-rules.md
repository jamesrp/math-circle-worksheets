## text
The keeper hides a red/yellow secret row. The breaker fills a test row with red/yellow counters.
Behind the folder, the keeper copies the test below the secret and puts a green triangle under each matching place.
The breaker records the number of triangles as the score. After a final guess, uncover the secret and check the scores.
