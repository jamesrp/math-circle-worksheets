## prompt
Play this code game with a partner, taking turns as keeper and breaker.
The keeper hides a secret behind the folder: two counters, each red or yellow.
The breaker builds a test in the next test row, with a red or yellow counter in every slot.
The keeper copies the test just below the secret, puts a green triangle under each place where the two colors match, and says how many triangles there are.
The breaker writes this score in the score box.
When the breaker says what the secret is, the keeper lifts the folder and you check every score together.
Find each secret with as few tests as you can.
