In the code game, the keeper hides a secret row of counters, each red or yellow, behind the folder.
The breaker builds a test in the next test row, with a red or yellow counter in every slot.
The keeper copies the test just below the secret, puts a green triangle under each place where the two colors match, and says how many triangles there are.
That number is the test's score, and the breaker writes it in the score box.
When the breaker says what the secret is, the keeper lifts the folder and every score is checked again.
A third player can be the referee, who watches every score being made.

\problem Your partner thinks of a whole number from 1 to 16.
Ask yes-or-no questions until you are sure of the number.
What is the smallest number of questions that always works?
Explain why fewer questions cannot always work.
Then answer the same questions for a number from 1 to 20.
