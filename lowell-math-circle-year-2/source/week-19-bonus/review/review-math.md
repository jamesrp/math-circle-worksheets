## Located mathematical ambiguity

**Grades 2–5, page 3, Problem 6.** Exact text: “Your partner must find its smallest working cards and use them as triggers.” “Smallest” can mean cards with the fewest symbols anywhere in the working collection, rather than every card that has no proper working subset. Those meanings have different mathematical answers. The rule immediately above in P5 is a counterexample: B is the globally smallest working card, but AC is also required as a trigger. B alone omits AC and ACD. Smallest fix: “Your partner must find **every working card with no smaller working card inside it**, and use those cards as triggers.” This preserves the reconstruction investigation rather than asserting a false reconstruction for the alternative reading.
