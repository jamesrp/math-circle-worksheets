## prompt
Lay two yarn routes from A to B with each pair of windings below. Make the number of places where the two strands cross each other as small as possible. Your partner tries to remove another of these crossings without changing either winding.

## text
Both ends are shared. Do not count A or B. Away from the ends, the strands must cross or stay apart: no shared pieces or other touches.
