## Mathematical destination and assumptions

Parts are positive integers and unordered; equal parts are indistinguishable. A canonical nonincreasing list represents one partition. Zero contributes no extra part; the empty partition is the optional n=0 boundary case. Physical groups must use all the units in the fixed total. The restricted collections "all parts odd" and "all sizes different" overlap, but neither contains the other. Equal counts do not mean they contain the same partitions.

Conjugation exchanges rows with columns in a left-aligned nonincreasing diagram. It preserves area, is its own inverse, and exchanges the largest row length with the number of nonempty rows. Hence it matches the at-most-k-parts and largest-part-at-most-k restrictions. This can be a concrete secondary direction, without introducing symmetric partitions or their hook theorem into this week's required core.

For odd/distinct rebuilding, join two equal parts q into 2q. Every join preserves the total and reduces the number of parts, so the process terminates. Fix an odd u. Every part has a unique form 2^j u, and joining within this family is binary carrying. The weighted count sum_j 2^j c_j is invariant for that odd u. At a terminal state all c_j are zero or one, so uniqueness of binary expansion gives exactly one possible terminal collection. Distinct odd cores cannot collide, by uniqueness of 2^j u. The inverse replaces 2^j u by 2^j copies of u; repeated equal halving does precisely that. Thus both maps undo each other, proving a bijection for every n, not merely a matching of the tested small catalogs. This explanation was independently reconstructed from the inspected mathematical source and does not require children to write binary digits.

Examples from the independent checker: (3,3,1,1,1) ↔ (6,2,1); (5,5,3,3,3,1,1) ↔ (10,6,3,2); nine ones ↔ (8,1); (5,3,1) stays unchanged. Unrestricted counts through 8 are 1,2,3,5,7,11,15,22, and odd/distinct counts from total 5 through 10 are 3,4,5,6,8,10. [checks.py](../../../../plans/new-themes-52-63/week-54/checks.py) generated [checks.json](../../../../plans/new-themes-52-63/week-54/checks.json): 9,296 partitions for n=0–25, both inverse identities, conjugation/involution and restricted-count identities; every legal equal-pair merge order was checked for odd partitions through n=16. Finite evidence supports the examples; the preceding invariant argument proves the general result. Neither establishes physical rehearsal or age fit.

## Operational recommendation

Recommend one combined Grades 2–5 packet with a physical grouping entry and readiness-dependent upper work. No forced K–1 packet. Younger children may form small groups orally with an adult, but the classification/bijection needs comparison of group sizes and retention of a reversible rule. Grades 4–5 additionally compare two whole collections and reason about reversibility; binary notation is unnecessary.
