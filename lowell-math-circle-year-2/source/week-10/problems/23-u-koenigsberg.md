## prompt
In the city of K\"onigsberg, seven bridges joined four areas of land, A, B, C and D. Can
someone walk through the city crossing every bridge exactly once? Explain. Where could the city
build one new bridge so that such a walk becomes possible? What is the smallest number of new
bridges the city needs for a walk that crosses every bridge exactly once and ends where it
started?
