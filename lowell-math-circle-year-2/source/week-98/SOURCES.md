## Mathematical source

Robert Morris, *Minimal percolating sets in bootstrap percolation*, introduction pp. 1–2 and Theorem 1 p. 3.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0702370

The source defines the two-neighbor process, distinguishes minimum from inclusion-minimal sets, and studies the latter. Our elementary perimeter proof, specific small instances and worksheet investigations are independently presented. Historical open questions in the paper are not asserted to remain open. The printed family is deterministic; it does not teach a random infinite-grid critical-threshold theorem.
