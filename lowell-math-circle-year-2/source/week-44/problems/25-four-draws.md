## prompt @0
Predict the chances of zero, one, two, three, or four red draws after four copying draws. Check your prediction exactly without making a 120-row list.

## figure
box_lines(0,4,18,8)

## key
Each of the five totals has 24 of the 120 marked histories, hence chance 1/5. Extend the six histories in each three-draw group by the five available fourth-draw identities. The contributions to totals 0 through 4 are:

0: 6 $\times$ 4, 0, 24.

1: 6 $\times$ 3, 6 $\times$ 1, 24.

2: 6 $\times$ 2, 6 $\times$ 2, 24.

3: 6 $\times$ 1, 6 $\times$ 3, 24.

4: 0, 6 $\times$ 4, 24.
