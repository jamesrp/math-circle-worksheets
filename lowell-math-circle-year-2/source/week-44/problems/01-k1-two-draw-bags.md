## prompt @5.5
Make every different bag you can reach after two draws. Which bags can come from two different draw stories?

## figure
k1_two_draw_bowls()

## key
After two draws the possible bags are \textbf{3R/1B} from RR, \textbf{2R/2B} from RB or BR, and \textbf{1R/3B} from BB. Only the middle bag has two different two-draw \emph{color} stories. This is a possibility question; counting these four words as equally likely would be wrong for random copying.
