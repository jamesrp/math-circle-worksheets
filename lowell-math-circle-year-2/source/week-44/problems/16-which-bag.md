## prompt @0
After four copying draws, which bag is more likely: five red and one blue, or three red and three blue?

## figure
which_bag()

## key
Neither: each has chance 1/5. RRRR has 1 $\times$ 2 $\times$ 3 $\times$ 4 = 24 of the 120 marked histories, since its red draws have 1, 2, 3 and 4 red counters to choose from. Each of the six 3R/3B color stories has 1 $\times$ 2 $\times$ 1 $\times$ 2 = 4, so that bag also has 24. An adult can carry the multiplication.
