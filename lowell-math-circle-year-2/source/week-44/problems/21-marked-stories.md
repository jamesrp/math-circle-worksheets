## prompt @10.5
Find all the two-draw marked stories. Use them to decide whether zero, one, or two red draws has the greatest chance.

## figure
box_lines(0,14,18,7,(22,2))

## key
The six marked histories are (R1,R1), (R1,R2), (R1,B1), (B1,R1), (B1,B1), (B1,B2). Each has probability 1/6. The first two have two red draws, the middle two have one, and the last two have zero. All totals are tied, two histories each.
