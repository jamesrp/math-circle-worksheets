# Week 24 kid-fit revision, November 6, 2026

On November 6, 2026 a kid-fit pilot revised the student pages (critic, then reviser, pages only) and re-rated them; the reviews are `plans/review/kid-fit/pilot/week-24/before-review-kids.md` and `after-review-kids.md` in the repository, and the summary is `plans/review/KID-FIT-PILOT.md`. James approved adopting the revised packets the same day. The approved pages were adopted unchanged: the problem files rebuild them byte for byte. This release is unpiloted; physical pretests have not been performed.

## Student pages

| Band | Before | After | Problems |
|---|---|---|---|
| K–1 | F24-K-v3, 8 pages | F24-K-v4, 9 pages | new P1; old P1–P8 are now P2–P9 |

| Grades 4–5 | F24-45-v3, 7 pages | F24-45-v4, 8 pages | new P1; old P1–P7 are now P2–P8 |

- **Shared rules** shortened: "Each card has the same chance. Mix the bags separately. Draw once from each bag, then put both cards back. The bigger number wins." The no-shared-number condition now sits in the problems that need it.
- **New Problem 1 in every band.** K–1 plays A against B, B against C and C against A for six rounds each, one counter per round in the winning deck's row. Grades 2–3 and 4–5 play pick first or pick second for ten rounds, with a score table (Grades 4–5 add a third player who draws and scores).
- **K–1:** the dot cards show values in a ten-frame and the cards are larger; P4 (C against A) adds a triangle with a box per side for the winning deck; P5 (advantage game) gets a counter track per game with a box for the chosen letter; P7 (swaps) gets a 3 × 3 grid of swap boxes to mark.
- **Grades 2–3:** P3 is now a hidden-bag guessing game (a partner hides two bags' letters, draws and says only which side won; guess after six rounds, then look); P6 asks only A against B for the six-card decks and adds all three four-card comparisons, where C now beats B 9 to 7.
- **Grades 4–5:** P5 asks only A's six-card chance against B, then all four-card chances.
- Cross-references now say "Problem 2" for the original decks.

## Mathematics checked (`facilitator-src/checks.py`, `src/check_answers.py`, `review/verify_kid_fit_2026_11_06.py`)

Each cycle edge wins a round with chance 5/9. Counted round by round from the actual decks:

| One game | Favored deck wins outright | Ties | Loses |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 rounds (K–1) | 45.3% (240,625/531,441) | 30.1% | 24.6% |
| 10 rounds (Grades 2–5) | 51.9% | 23.1% | 25.0% |

These confirm the figures in the pilot summary (45% and 52%). In the K–1 Problem 1 games, all three favored decks win only 9.3% of the time (about 1 in 11), and some deck wins both its games 33.4% of the time. In Grades 2–3 Problem 3, guessing the side that won more rounds is right 60.3% of the time after six rounds and 63.4% after ten. Six-card A against B is 20 to 16 of 36; four-card A over B 10–6, C over A 10–6, C over B 9–7. The five winning swaps are exactly the swaps in which A gives the larger card.
