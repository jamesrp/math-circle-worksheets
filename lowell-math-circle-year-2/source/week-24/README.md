**Adult-read fix, November 7, 2026.** An adult cold-read pilot (`plans/review/ADULT-READ-PILOT.md`) found that a parent volunteer reading the pages alone could not tell what Problem 1 was in any band: K–1 page 1 named bags, decks and games before any page showed what was in a deck, and the Grades 2–3 and 4–5 First pick / Second pick table did not say what to record. James approved a fix the same day. The shared rules now say "Decks A, B and C are bags of three number cards, shown below" and every band's page 1 pictures the three decks (K–1 as dot cards in a row above Problem 1's game); K–1 Problem 1 says to play with a partner and names the three games; the pick-first table's first row is a shaded example, "A, 4 wins" and "C, 6 wins" (a first try, "A 4, C 6", was read by a fresh adult reader as card values). Only page 1 of each band changed; problem numbering is unchanged. Packets F24-K-v5 (9 pages), F24-23-v4 (8) and F24-45-v5 (8); the guide's page 2 names them. A one-page plain adult table card, [week-24-adult-card.pdf](../../week-24/week-24-adult-card.pdf) (source `adult-card/adult-card.tex`, built with `pdflatex`), gives the activity, how to make the decks, the launch, what Problem 1 is for and the key answers. Unpiloted. The replaced PDFs, ZIP and problem files are in the `archive-before-adult-read-2026-11-07/` folders here and in the print folder.

**Kid-fit revision, November 6, 2026.** A kid-fit pilot revised the student pages (critic, then reviser, pages only; reviews in `plans/review/kid-fit/pilot/week-24/`), and James approved adopting them on November 6, 2026; the problem files here rebuild the approved packets byte for byte. Packets F24-K-v4 (9 pages), F24-23-v3 (8) and F24-45-v4 (8).
