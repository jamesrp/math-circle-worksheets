# Sources and adaptation boundaries

These owned boards, tasks and guides are new teaching adaptations. No external worksheet, book, blog illustration, video frame, or poster is included. Mathematical mechanisms credited below are established results, not new research solutions.

Joel David Hamkins, [Counting to Infinity and Beyond](https://jdh.hamkins.org/counting-to-infinity-poster/) (September 2, 2021): ordinal visualizations toward ω². We use original queue/matching boards, and give all order-type arguments in the guide. No poster image is republished. Concatenation here is ordinary ordinal arithmetic, distinct from surreal field addition.

Teaching design: Natasha Rozhkovskaya, *Math Circles for Elementary School Students*, Lesson 3 “At the lesson” item 1 (try before organizing possibilities), Lesson 7 item 1 (missed legal game moves), Lesson 8 item 2 (slow copying); and Givental, Nemirovskaya and Zakharevich, *Math Circle by the Bay*, preface pp. viii–x (accessible depth, materials, varied pace). The guide identifies the relevant observation and our proposed response. These sources do not validate the new activities. Authorized local originals were read; no reference books are redistributed.
