## prompt
Cut out the two wheels on the last two pages and join them through the middle dots with a brass fastener. Setting the wheel to D means turning it until the inner A is next to the outer D. Each letter of a message on the inner wheel goes into code as the outer letter next to it, so with the wheel set to D, CAT goes into code as FDW. These codes were made with settings nobody wrote down. Find the messages, and find two different words that the last code could have come from.
