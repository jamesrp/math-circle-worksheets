# Week 4: Ring shifts and perfect shuffles

Revised September 27, 2026, applying the [Week 1 classroom guidance](../../../../plans/week-01-classroom-review.md). **All v3 revisions are unpiloted.** The [weekly plan](../../../../plans/week-04-redesign.md) distinguishes source lessons, classroom evidence from Week 1, and design proposals for this week. ## Current print files

- [K–1](../../../week-04/archive/week-04-k-1.pdf) — 3 pages, F04-K-v3. Several starts with one/two/three-hop rules, then hidden-turn and undo tests.
- [Grades 2–3](../../../week-04/archive/week-04-grades-2-3.pdf) — 3 pages, F04-M-v3. Concrete shift outputs, paired +2 routes, +3/+5 contrasts, and an inverse from four starts.
- [Grades 4–5](../../../week-04/archive/week-04-grades-4-5.pdf) — 4 pages, F04-U-v3. +4/+8 and +3/+6/+5 experiments before classification, an 18-ring, distance records and inverses.
- [Optional extra, roughly grades 6–7](../../../week-04/archive/week-04-extra-grades-6-7.pdf) — 3 pages, F04-X-v3. Real rows and card tracks before a position map, loops, doubling and deck comparisons.
- [Facilitator](../../../week-04/archive/week-04-facilitator.pdf) — 5 pages, F04-FAC-v3. Whole-group launch, prerequisites, flexible hour, stopping choices, full proofs, solutions for added examples, source lineage and observation prompts.

**Start small:** for the example roster, print the first page for each child: three K, three middle and one upper = **seven starting sheets**. Keep continuation masters ready and copy selected later pages as needed; retain earlier working mats when referenced. The extra may stay digital until chosen. Full packets total 13 student pages across the four levels, plus the 5-page guide; these are choices and workspace, not a one-hour completion target.

Print US Letter, preferably single-sided and Actual Size. Grade labels are approximate; use reading, counting and reasoning prerequisites in the guide. Read-aloud, adult scribing and physical demonstrations are valid entry points.

**Materials:** Seven counters, paper and pencils; cards 0–7 (8–15 for the larger-deck reserve).

The shared sequence allows handling materials first, then one brief whole-group legal attempt and demonstrated record, before different starting tasks. Later representations are introduced after the concrete comparisons that give them a purpose. The mathematical explanations remain in the guide for adult conversation when children are ready.

## Build and check

Run `sh lowell-math-circle-year-2/source/week-04/build.sh` from the repository root. The build runs `verify.py`, compiles all five TeX files twice, and copies PDFs to `lowell-math-circle-year-2/week-04/`. Intermediates stay in `tmp/pdfs/week-04-build/`. Requires Python 3 and the existing pdfLaTeX/TikZ setup; no new runtime is needed.

The independent mathematical checker includes the new v3 examples as well as the original exhaustive checks. [REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md) records rendered-page QA and its limits. Record only activities actually encountered in the shared [use log](../../../../plans/fall-k-5-year-a-use-log.md), with exact examples and whether results were tried, conjectured, checked in cases, proved or supplied.
