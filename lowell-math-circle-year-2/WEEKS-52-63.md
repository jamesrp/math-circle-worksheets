## Routes and relationships

- **Week 52:** Changing shapes → local braces → row/column links → minimum bracing and count limits. Revised October 23, 2026 (F52-S-v3): K–1 pairs continue to Problems 4–5 on their own 2-by-2 kit, and Problem 13 names six different cells.
- **Week 53:** Buy a connected network → remove loops → improve by exchange → certify minimum cost and examine ties. Revised October 23, 2026 (W53-networks-v2): the page 6 and 8 price labels sit on their own links, and K–1 pairs work pages 1–4, whose prices all have dots.
- **Week 54:** Small strip catalogs → odd/distinct matching and inverse → join-order independence; a separate row/column investigation. Revised October 23, 2026: K–1 has an adult-led route through pages 1–3 on guide p. 2 with two 12-cube kits, the kits are linking cubes, and Problem 7's answer grids match the input squares.
- **Week 55:** Distinct sums → the smallest possible sumset → explain the bound and recover equal spacing from equality. Revised October 23, 2026: K–1 has an adult-led route through pages 1–3 with dot cards on guide p. 2, the input rule says A and B may share numbers, and the page 8 grid lines no longer cross the numbers.
- **Week 56:** Assembled incidences and corner fans → total angular gap → surface redrawings and Euler's formula. Since October 23, K–1 has a route through Problems 1 and 3–5 by marks, fans and paper gap copies (pages headed K–5).
- **Week 57:** Compare area, inside dots and boundary dots → lattice-polygon rule → joining, subdivision and holes. Revised October 23, 2026: K–1 has an adult-led four-square route on guide p. 3, the area pieces are printed on two materials pages, and the student pages say the area check once and stack Problem 3's boards.
- **Week 58:** Whole-card division → divisible strips and cut-and-choose → indivisibility → proportionality versus envy-freeness. Revised October 23, 2026 (W58-shared-v3): K–1 pairs follow an adult-led route on pages 1, 2 and 5 (pages 1 and 5 headed K–5), and the strips print whole at 240 mm.
- **Week 59:** Measure while turning between parallel supports → noncircular constant width → explain the support geometry. Revised October 23, 2026 (W59-S-v3): K–1 pairs follow an adult-led route on page 2 (headed K–5), sorting the pieces between rails closed on the disk, and Problem 6's generating circle is now a held hint in the guide.
- **Week 60:** Irreversible take/pass play → complete equally weighted cases → continuation values, changing thresholds and optimality. Revised October 23, 2026 (W60-S-v3, eight pages): K–1 children follow an adult-led route on pages 1, 2 and 6 (headed K–5), playing two-offer rounds and comparing take and pass cube towers; offers come from a stickered die by default; and Problem 7 adds the bag 0, 2, 6, whose middle ticket is passed.
- **Week 61:** Surface routes and corners → great circles and shortest arcs → spherical triangles, lunes and excess area. Since October 23, K–1 has an adult-led route with string and straws on guide p. 3, and the packet is W61-S-v4.
- **Week 62:** Minimum slots → odd cycles → first-fit order and repair → counts with named slots. Since October 23, counters mark slots by colour, K–1 has an adult-led counter route on pp. 1–2 (guide p. 4), cards and headers print from a materials sheet, and the packet is W62-S-v2.
- **Week 63:** Card-to-home assignments → overlapping forbidden events → complete derangement counts and reversible recurrence cases. Since October 23, K–1 has a route through Problems 1–3 on guide p. 3, and the cards' pictures sit beside the home letters.
